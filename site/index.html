Build one with me

You handle the code, handmade or AI-assisted, both welcome. I handle the design, art, audio, writing, marketing and publishing. Revenue is split equally among the core team.

How it works

1. Browse the designs and pick one you'd love to build. Money is a fine motivation too.

2. Email me (fastest) or find me on Discord, with something that shows your coding.

3. We work as a remote introvert team, with no deadlines. Once there's a playable prototype, we bring in more people.

Monetization depends on the project, but it always stays ethical. The game's first job is to serve the player.

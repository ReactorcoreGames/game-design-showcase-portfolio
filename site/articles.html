The thinking behind the designs

Every design in this portfolio rests on the ideas in these articles: that games are satisfiers of real human needs, that a game can keep giving through an infinite metagame instead of ending, and that the industry's defaults are worth questioning.

They're long-form and opinionated, written for designers, developers, employers, and anyone who wants games to be more than a way to pass time. Each one opens as a Google Doc.

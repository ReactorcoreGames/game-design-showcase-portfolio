How I work

  • Design comes firstI start from the human need a game meets and build the systems that meet it. I prefer infinite metagames to throwaway content, and multiplicative content to additive.
  • I direct production end to endI run the production of my own projects through Claude Code, covering code, tools, content pipelines and these very pages. A design from me comes from someone who knows what it costs to build.
  • A generalist behind the designerI make pixel art, 3D and voxel models, sound edits, promo art and video, writing, community work and publishing. When the team has a gap, I can usually fill it or brief whoever does.
  • I listen to feedback properlyI'm versed in Nonviolent Communication, so I read feedback for the need behind it, not just the words.

# AAL Lore, Basics
(This document explains the basic established rules of the ingame universe in my games.)

Overall the tone of the game's universe is grounded/realistic and it tries to aim for a believable world, with some softer edges. While it is a grounded scifi universe, its not a hardcore ultra-realistic world, just believable enough for a non-expert layman person to be like "yeah, that could make sense to me on some level".

Technology wise, there is FTL, advanced power sources, megastructures, artificial intelligence, sentient machines, advanced automation and industry, highly developed society and social systems, full sustainability on a galactic scale, enourmous superweapons with planetary scale destructive potential, biological immortality, advanced biomods amd augments, impressive genetical and biological engineering. In some regards, its a soft utopia with a happy populace with a bright future and an optimistic outlook, but with occasional dangers and challenges that never truly offer 100% safety.

However, there is no teleportation in this universe and FLT works only in proportion to how far away they are from large sources of gravity, like planets or stars - slowing down to a crawl the closer a FTL capable vessel comes to one, being mostly useless below the high orbit of a the equivalent of a moon sized celestial object; forcing ships to use conventional thrust or other mobility technologies to move about near and on a planet's surface or its atmosphere. It also renders any FLT based planetary bombardment weapons completely null and void, as FTL acceleration uses entirely different laws of nature compared to regular physics momentum and acceleration in this ingame universe.

Magic and mana does not exist at all, not in the sense of a traditional fantasy sense. However, psychic abilities like telekinesis and similar extrasensory perception abilites do exist, but are exceedingly rare and require exceedingly expensive, complex and delicate connections to the world's astral realm, a parallel dimension that is intervowen with the physical world, being sort of a limbo space where souls stays when they're not occupying a living body in the physical world due to a recent death. The astral realm is like an neutral amorphous soup that exists everywhere at the same time and is the place where souls go after a living being dies and from which a soul or multiple souls are deposited into a new being that is born in the physical world. The astral realm is like the aloof brains of the universe that act as the will of the universe, but lacks any sort of actual intelligence, operating merely on instinct. Those with an exceedingly strong enough spiritual connection to nature/cosmos may be able to tap into this will of the universe to cause changes within the physical world, manifesting as various psychic abilities.

The structure of the game's greater universe is also unique; its both finite and infinite at the same time - if it were visualized as a simplified 2D plane, it would look like a regular round bowl with a big wide flat area in the middle and then curved edges around it. The center area is the universe proper and is mindbogglingly wide, contains many superclusters that contain staggering numbers of galaxies within. A big bang event is only a ultra-rare occurance that only happens in very extreme cases and even then only ever affects a smaller portion of the universe, meaning big bang events are just a function of the universe rather than how it started. Instead, the universe was never truly born from any particular event such as the big bang, instead it simply has always existed without a beginning and without end, it simply exists as a fact and law of nature. Somewhere in this universe the milkyway galaxy and the solar system exists, mirroring our own real life counterpart. From the edges of this flat area, surrounding it, begins a curve that extends to infinity, starting gentle and eventually becoming a steep vertical 90' angle after several thousand or million lightyears. This curved area is a called the "wilderness" where ejected material from the flat base universe may eventually end up and may drift within the wilderness, often simply going in like a skateboarder into a quarter-pipe ramp, reversing its momentum and drifting/flying back into the universe proper. While in the wilderness, the ejected material may possibly colliding with other material, it may become reshuffled or mutated by the innate storms of the wilderness that are endlessly more intense the deeper up the curve something manages to travel or end up temporarily dissappearing in an anomaly wormhole, only to be spit out somewhere in the universe proper if a spacetime rift is opened due to an extreme high energy event or an extreme void event where a portion of a universe became so barren of any material that it forces a space-time rift to eject new material into it from the wilderness. Just to clarify, I'm using the 2D plane and bowl as a simplified metaphor, when the universe is 3D, and the bowl is more of a sphere with an inner stable zone and an outer wilderness zone that pushes ejected things back into the stable zone. Its a fully equivalent exhange based world that endlessly recycles itself - no matter is ever created or destroyed, only changes in state. The stable universe is only stable in name ofcourse, stars still due their whole life cycle thing, physics still govern the movement of celestial bodies and so forth, the inner stable zone is always in flux, evolving, changing, being as a whole a living breathing universe.

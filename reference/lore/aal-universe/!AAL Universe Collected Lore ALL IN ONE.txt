Color coding:
Each branch of the civilization's vehicles, machines, objects, structures, items, uniforms, clothing, markings and systems would have a unique universal 2-3 color code (and any optional pattern) to identify the role / purpose / branch a thing belongs to.

First, a primary color(s) denote the general branch of operation and theme. 80%

Then, a secondary color denotes the specific sub category of this branch. 20%

Thirdly, an ornamental pattern, shape or texture denotes the specific object type of that sub-branch.

Lastly, a logo, insignia, name, numbering, id, suit patch and flag communicate the full description of the branch.

All are as stylish is possible, all are respected equally and all are different, yet cooperative and cohesive.

Full color of the rainbow is used. Attempt is made to give some level of relatability for quicker recognition.

Nature of colors themselves is taken into consideration when assigning them to purposes; muted natural colors are used in more general purposes, while garish, unnatural colors are used for when visibility matters or when attention is evoked.

--- AAL Universe Collected Lore/AAL FTL travel plan.txt ---
Star on surface of planet.

Launch from ship dock parking. Reach it via local transit systems. Parking is divided by size classes.

Connect to skyway: Fly ship into appropriate size lane via skyway entry funnel from parking. Skyway is a highway with entries and exits. The initial size class funnel sorts the ship into the correct lane and this lane connects to the larger skyway highway.

Edit: the size sorting funnel is actually only present at the edge of the star system, where FTL arrivals / departures happen. Otherwise ships stay in their respective size lanes throughout the whole star system starway and skyway road networks.

Edit2: there may be additional purpose / theme driven sorting for various types of traffic, priority lanes vs regular lanes (military, medical, rescue, police, security, emergency), maintenance lanes (drones, technicians).

Edit3: it is possible to eject and reconnect to a lane at almost any time. Simply fly up to leave and drift or hover, and to reconnect receive an ok signal from the traffic controller to fly and reconnect back into the lane. Free flight in dense traffic homeworlds with fully operational sky/starways will result in arrest. Frankly, starways / skyways are not only fastest, safest and coordinated but also the most convenient way to travel in a busy environment where a single mishap could result in catastrophic damage and loss of life.

Edit4: Ejecting is useful for taking a break, address a problem or checking the map for route and destination information.

Edit5: skyway / starway traffic is controlled, monitored and organized by joint effort of ship ai autopilots, starway infrastructure nodes, drones, hubs, controllers, satellites and hyperwave comms. There are standards for clearances between ships, tradelane spacing, skyway/starway spacing, intervals of buoys, drone bays (maintenance and security), hyperwave commsats, intermediate traffic controller stations, supply depots and ship condition checking sensors. Lots of redundancy and self checking.

Edit6: manual piloting in homeworld-class worlds is too bothersome, stressful and dangerous. The ai can provide No Man Sky tier on-rails controls which are mostly automated, but full manual control is not advised; doing so will result in getting kicked out of the skyway / starway and promptly getting arrested by drones.

Edit7: during star system wide emergency disaster evacuation, all ships are to fly directly up if they're travelling clockwise and down if flying counterclockwise. After this, escaped ships link up and organize into a fleet a and b for up and down ships. They may merge or travel separately. Each ship will estimate which evacuation route is most suitable and will either fly up or use the tradelane to reach the edge of the system, allowing them to fully utilize their FTL drive. Upward flying ships from the habzone will need roughly 5 hours to reach the edge before proper FTL kicks in. Tradelane evacuees can get out in 1 hour or less.

Edit8: signage: apart from satellites, nodes, controllers, maintenance depots, drone bays, hubs and tradelane segments, most of starways are devoid of any visual aids, signage or markings. These are provided by augmented reality AR locally to each ship that requests them. Hyperwave nodes relay latest information and traffic data / stop and go signals and the ai onboard a ship displays this info as AR content to passengers. The only things that are visible in space are collision warning lights, faint zone markings for station / celestial territory and docking lights / holograms.

Edit8b: in orbit, atmo and surface, markings, lights and holograms are more common. For mass transit and public spaces markings are abundant and redundant.

Fly the skyway lane and turn to the fork that takes to orbit. Otherwise continue to another continental destination and land there.

Fly via skyway all the way up to orbit. Continue via orbital skyway to an orbital destination, otherwise take a fork to leave the orbit to reach the greater habzone. Now entering the starway.

At the habzone, continue to circle the habzone and reach another planet, station, colony or megastructure. 

Note: During straight long empty portions of a starway, segments of in-system alternative externally induced form of FTL system called a tradelane, consisting of regularly spaced projector-linkers generate and create a magnetic tunnel route corridor, where ships will be externally sucked, locked and slid from start of the track to the end of the track by a different form of FTL than that of interstellar ship FTL travel is, which is internally induced.

Note2: This tradelane is effective inside star systems, but not practical or useful for interstellar travel, for which ship FTL drives are better and more powerful.

Note3: Unlike ship FTL travel, which works by long acceleration followed by short deceleration, tradelane FTL works by quick boost to its maximum possible FTL speed, keeping the velocity constant, then quick return to regular non-FTL speed after the exit. It sort of works like a vacuum cleaner pressure tube that sucks, moves and plops out ships out the other end.

Note4: Also unlike ship FTL, tradelane FTL is less visually spectacular, being more similar to mass effect or star   wars style sudden poof and pop FTL entry and exit.

Otherwise, take a turn at a fork to leave the habzone. This way will lead to the main defense line and past that, the outer habzone ship parking zone and the long range tradelane entry towards the edge of the star system, with a midway checkpoint (usually this is where the farthest significant natural celestial body like pluto would be).

Either turn to enter parking (size sensitive, you're still in a lane of your own ship size class S, M, L or XL) or turn to enter the long range tradelane, a freelancer style-ish alternative FTL transit system which is merely just specialized segments / portions of the usual starway.

The long-range tradelane is a segmented network of two-way routes. First sector is the zone between star habzone and midway. There may be other destinations before midway point where the secondary defense line lies, such as remnant celestials, special bases, zones or other unusual locations. Either travel to any of these sub locations or continue to midway.

At midway, another parking zone is present, as well as the secondary defense line, along with a major military checkpoint fortress station. Other minor stations, platforms and satellites may be here too. Like previously, midway to edge is the second sector, where one can reach other remnant celestials, special bases, zones or other unusual locations via two-way tradelanes. Either travel to any of these sub locations or continue to edge.

At edge, there is another parking zone, a large military border station, a sorting funnel for incoming FTL traffic, an exit location to an FTL launch zone, aka the "cup" which has 5 inbound and outbound cells (left, right, up, down, forward) for ships to slot themselves in or out of. 

For outgoing ships, there is an enormous mass relay like FTL launch catapult tesla coil like device that momentarily latches on to launching ships with a plasma beam like tether and impart a momentum boost into them, allowing them to reach max FTL speeds faster. For incoming ships, this same mass relay like megastructure can latch and absorb their momentum, slowing incoming ships to a crawl.

Still at the edge, the tertiary defense line is located here. There is also an edge circling tradelane to reach the other cups / border stations of the star system. In a fully developed homeworld, there are always 6 FTL entry and exit cups: up, down, left, right, forward, backward. They're the sanctioned entry/exit points to a star system. Trying to enter or exit a homeworld class system through other means than the cups and sorting funnels will raise an alarm and prompt automated retaliation response from military drones and ships.

Besides the circling tradelane, there is also some automated outposts and perhaps even other fringe space stations, mini-celestials, platforms and other satellites / arrays at the edge perimeter.

From here, one can enter exit the starway to enter the cup area, where you can plot a destination that is accessible (in the range of angle of the particular cup you're in), connect with the FTL traffic controller to recieve a slot and a timeframe to launch from, then proceed to blast away to your far away destination via ship internal FTL drive, which is fully operational and effective outside the edge of the solar system.

Alternatively, especially if you're flying a smaller vessel than the XL size class, you may connect to a larger XL size "rack ship" similar to a rollercoaster ride and its associated loading process.

This mothership / ark / rack ship provides faster FTL travel, because ship / engine size dictates max FTL speeds, acceleration and range. It is especially helpful to smaller S, M and L class vessels for this reason to piggyback on a rack ship.

Depending on expected participants, demand, schedule and timeframe, the rackship gets loaded up as much as it makes sense for the mentioned factors taken into consideration. If theres too little outgoing traffic, then no rackship travel will be done and the few participants will need to fly manually themselves. Otherwise its a waste of energy and resources. Kanban methods apply here.

Anyway, if the FTL destination is another fully developed homeworld class star system, then upon entry into the FTL cup's slot that was assigned on FTL launch at the original star system (and getting decelerated by the local mass relay catapult), you will proceed to the size class sorting funnel (while screened by the local border station checkpoint for security purposes) and enter the local starway again to travel to other destinations within this new solar system.

For FTL travel in the galaxy, if the destination is relatively near with no obstacles or intersections, then a direct path will be plotted to it. However for long distance travel from one galactic sector to anothet, a more vertical wide arc hop is made instead. For relative close destination clusters which have some intersecting FTL traffic, then the otherwise direct paths are slightly bent below or above each other to prevent FTL collisions between stars.

Also for skyway, starway and FTL traffic, in and out lanes are rotated so both are facing up. (First thought to flip opposite lane to be upside down, but then remember that I cant do that for a planet's skyway for gravity reasons, whoops).

In a skyway and starway, ship size classes go from biggest at bottom, while smallest at top.

Other worlds.

Not all worlds are fully developed and most do not have full starway infrastructure in place. Maybe there is no infrastructure at all. Maybe there wont ever be if that particular star system is not intended for habitation due to special circumstances. Like an unstable star or the area is designated as disposable (like the firezone star system).

For partial worlds, cups are often shortly developed once a stronger presence is established on at least one major celestial in the solar system. Defence infrastructure, starway definement and tradelanes follow them later, along with more expansion and habzone development. This is later complemented with further megastructures, sun stabilization and dyson swarm construction. Either way, cups are important if traffic to and from the solar system is going to be busy.

For solar systems with no infrastructure at all, ships will rely on hyperwave scans; first 360 around then in all axis to determine other surrounding FTL travellers that may intersect with their own path essentially creating a ping of their own location and intent while doing so. Then a forward vector fine narrow hyperwave scan is made to detect obstacles in the flight path. If all good, a relatively safe FTL passage can be made, with the unlikely possibility of a freak accident or deliberate trap by complicated circumstances. To eliminate even this risk, chartered routes between homeworlds have been pre-cleared and are monitored for any disturbances.

Infact, it is standard protocol that anyone that is intending to travel to a new uncharted solar system, the local or any nearest fully developed homeworld star will launch two autonomous and self-sufficient hyperwave nodes, one to top of the star system and another at the bottom (so that no matter where someone is, they can have a direct line of contact with at least one of the two nodes).

These nodes will automatically set up themselves at the location, just at the edges of the uncharted solar system and remain there forever or until a homeworld class starway infrastructure replaces them.

The nodes will scan the new uncharted solar system, noting any significant celestials, any major or unusual activity, any lifesigns or civilizations, any hazards or instabilities or hostiles.

Clearing that, the node will then scan the path from the uncharted system to the location where the willing explorer is, as well as other convenient routes nearby from other homeworld class solar systems. It is more patient and thorough with its scans and may yield information on the quality and sustainability of the route that a ship on its own wouldn't want to bother with. It will more or less standardize the path, making it safer and clearer. If it detects obstructions or other problems with a route, either now or later, it'll give a signal to a nearest homeworld star to send autonomous drones to tidy up the route and fix any issues with it.

This entire process takes time and depends on what is encountered in the new area. The initial launch of the 2 nodes may take an hour to get to their locations. For the local scans, 1-7 days may pass, for the route scans another 2-3 weeks may go by. If problems with route are found, the drones may take a day, week or months before solving the issue.

If the explorer is in a hurry and willing to jump into the unknown and possibly bare the dangers that lie ahead, then they can do an arcing vertical hop FTL flight to the uncharted solar system after using their ship's hyperwave curve vector scanner. It is never advised, but it is possible to go straight away. Its just a gamble, that's all.

On the whole though, in the lack of stationary communication installations either locally or in nearby stars, ships cooperate and coordinate with each other via their own hyperwave communicators. Some ships can take a higher role of being a relay or a controller for other ships, though usually its all decentralized, where each unit cares for its own vicinity and status yet as a whole it all just seems to work in harmony. Technically any stationary installations / controllers simply take the load off others and do the thinking and monitoring for others, so that they can conserve energy and bandwidth.

Upon entry to an uncharted solar system at any point in its edge, ships can freely fly anywhere they want, simply avoiding fellow ships and obstacles dynamically with active collision detection. Since FTL inside a star system is limited to 100-300K km/s (aka slower than FTL), this is now considerably easier.

Joining orbits and landing on planets is free game and again, done dynamically to avoid colliding with other stuff. Its like old-school driving a bicycle; keep your head on a swivel, avoid crashing at all times, stay balanced and don't be too rash / reckless. Its stressful, chaotic and mostly disorganized (the exception being fleet formations), but it does reduce the risk of accidents.

For atmospheres that a civilization wants to preserve (read: not ruin), there may be less than 10000 (30 per day) medium ships per year landing / taking off on said planet. Anymore than that, and a skyway construction with a vertical vacuum corridor, excess atmosphere reservoirs and a proper ship parking facility at the end of the corridor need to be built for the sustainability of the planet and its atmosphere. Otherwise the thruster exhaust, noise, light, re-entry friction, wind generation by a significant amount of ship traffic will start to affect the environment quite seriously. Alternatively, atmosphere cleaners, purifiers and coolers are necessary to mitigate said damages with a vacuum corridor entry.

In galactic FTL travel, if there are many intersecting routes and a vast empty void between stars, a connecting hub may be constructed there, consisting of a condensed solar system model with the usual 6 FTL cup set up, complete with mass relays, a space station in the center a reverse tradelane tech field to simulate a star's gravity bubble effect to cancel / slow FTL flyers quickly and conveniently. Since dark voids in a galaxy are a source of richer higgs boson energy wells, these hubs also have another good reason to exist and house various civilizational activity, despite seemingly being in the middle of nowhere.



Ship FTL process:
. Start at 0km/s, front shield on, engine on, go.
. Accelerate to 10.000km/s, activate FTL drive ramjet
. Blast off + cone shield plasma lance activation.
. Hypervelocity FTL acceleration is go.
. Calculate exit point countdown ETA.
. Brilliant engine circular wake, cone shield shines.
. Ship is bathes in an rainbow color shifting spectacular FTL field, depending on current speed.
. Keep acceleration going until ETA zero.

At ETA zero:
. At this point acceleration is max.
. FTL drive shutdown / suspend.
. Ship begins to rapidly lose speed, going back towards the beginning 100.000km/s.
. Ship will still drift for a while as it looses speed and will arrive at destination with correct speed precisely
. While slowing down, lance cone shines gradually less, while ships FTL momentum field is shifting its colors in backwards order, engine wake gradually shrinks to normal size.
. At FTL speed exit, cone lance vanishes, regular frontal impact shield is back visible again. Last of ships FTL momentum field disappears with muted color, engine wake back to normal.
. Regular slowdown to 0km/s. Stop.


FTL technicality:
The FTL drive essentially creates a condition, field, phase mode that immerses or wraps the ship. This field allows vessel to ignore the limitation of lightspeed cap. Its a limit canceller. It creates, builds and maintains this field when its on and certain conditions are met, such as the minimum speed of 100.000km/s and an ongoing engine burn. Without these there is no achieving FTL speeds.

If its turned off while in FTL flight, then the conditions for an FTL field to stay sustainable are impaired, as FTL drive is off and it is no longer built and maintained. At this point, the field starts to dissipate and gradually vanish. 

It does not do so instantly, so the ship will continue to drift at high FTL speeds for a potentially very long distance, with larger ships and higher already achieved FTL speed having tougher residual fields that will take longer to dissipate than those on smaller or slower vessels.

The FTL drive can be turned back on at any time, that's totally fine. If just the engine burn is stopped, then the field will remain strong, but will start to gradually get weaker, albeit at a far slower pace than the FTL drive shutdown. In this case, after a long while the speed will eventually decrease below the minimum threshold and the ships FTL drive won't be effective any more.

This residual field is equal to the FTL speed and will be gone entirely once sub-ftl speeds have been achieved (or the 100.000km/s minimum, still open which one I'll use). Again, at that point the ship is now cruising or drifting at the same velocity it started before going FTL. The mass relay catapult can absorb this normal speed and slow the ship down to a halt. In the case of a hub FTL stop, the reverse tradelane tech suspends the ships movement to a crawl (like deadspace stasis) at which the catapult will dispel the FTL field and strip the remaining normal velocity as just mentioned.

Because of this, the onboard ship ai will use its navigational computer to calculate how long to accelerate, how powerfully to accelerate and at what point to turn off the FTL drive so that at end of the dissipation drift, the ship would precisely arrive at the correct location. These values will differ for each ship size class, engine type and flight strength (full power rapid or slow steady gain).

Outside of FTL movement, the ship has powerful sublight propulsion drive and a frontal field ram shield that protects from in-path collisions. Below is an excerpt taken from the 'AAL: Superweapon' game's lore:

...the game's story takes place at this time, at the area of one such war laboratory complex that spans two star systems, one as the stable manufacturing and development complex spread over multiple planets, stations and colonies, while another one as a remote testing firezone with numerous mostly desolate, barren planets populated by the occasional outpost and orbiting monitoring space stations.

The Chaos Management System:

The truth is that what happened, was for the first time ever, a weapon so ridiculously powerful was fired that it broke the limit of how much action the universe can handle at a time, causing it to reveal one of its more fundamental, severe countermeasures to heal the damage caused by the overkill caused by the superweapon. Its as if the weapon was so absurdly powerful and incorrectly configured that it caused a massive wound in the fabric of space-time itself.

This is a thing called the "chaos management system" that is unique to this particular (game) universe. Also called "Chaos System" for short. While "Harmony System" would be a more accurate name for it due to what its true purpose is, the fact that it bring about harmony through violent chaotic reactions and storm-like events makes the "Chaos System" feel more appropriate from the observer's point of view.

Commonly in video games in real life, I've discovered as a player that some games were designed with the mentality of no restrictions, assuming that technology would improve in the future infinitely and thus something that is laggy today, would be smooth on newer, more advanced hardware. From my experience this is a terrible idea and should not be done. In Cortex Command, the game was only ever really able to support a very small scale type of gameplay with a small number of objects that can exist on the level at any given time. Sadly, the developers (and by extention, the modders too) created gameplay and scenarios where these limits were doomed to be crossed, if the game was to be played seriously to any degree. 

Players would automatically end up in situations where their frames per second were down incredibly low numbers, below 10 fps and sometimes even less than 1 fps, turning the whole experience into a frustrating slideshow. As a game developer, I've been seeking to find a way to create a platform-- a universe where one could have all manner of amazing epic things happen fluently without it lagging all to hell. 

The solution was is what I named the "Chaos Management System", which in a nutshell essentially intervenes with the simulation by implementing certain clear consequences when the game is approaching or find itself in a situation that is beyond the means that the computer's hardware is capable of handling. The different actions range from subtle ones like unloading unimportant objects into the background, miniziming complex objects into simpler ones, grouping similar individual objects into a group entity, slowing down advanced processes to take more time like AI decisions and other similar opportunities like this. Returning back to the game's events, the superweapon was improperly configured and spawned too many particles at once or used a needlessly expensive algorithm to create its blast. Because of its enourmous size and the enourmous energies involved, the resulting chaos rift ended up being so large that it enveloped both star systems.

The superweapon causes a huge mess, leaving two star system enveloped in this strange miasma / field / cloud left by the chaos rift event and now the lab personnel would like to go back, clean up the mess, salvage what is still intact, remove any sources of danger, gather information on what the heck just happened, rescue anyone trapped within, resolve any dangerous crisis situations that may occur from erratic foreign beings having access to superweapon prototypes and rebuild the facilities so that the work can continue.

PART OF THE UNIVERSE
====================

Clump System
At the lowest chunk level that by maximum go down to 10m2 in size (32px/m standard, so 320px, so quarter of screen on 1280x wide resolution), digging / cutting beyond this point or beyond the point of a certain matter density value, the chunk will break down into clumps - small 1 meter or 1/2 meter balls, squares, triangles with the color and texture overlay determined by composition + possible paint/pigment.
(maybe 8m2 or 4m2 though?)
The small clumps are the smallest possible unit of solid terrain mass. However they are also the least stable and will automatically fuse into the surrounding terrain or air or nebula or field or sector if left unattended. Effectively they get pressed by gravity, radiation, entropy, microbacteria, nature, erosion and weather to dissolve back into surrounding chunks.

Chunks System 8m-100m
Larger than clumps, chunks are the most prominent and somewhat stable form of terrain / liquid / gas / plasma / other matter in the world at player level. Each chunk may be a square, circle or triangle and may overlap with other chunks. If they do overlap, then they're fused or connected to a larger mass entity, like a region.

NOTE-Player view is usually 1280x1024, so 40 meters at default level, while 80 meters at max zoom out (2x vision when human, any vision past this range is mipmapped and simplified or done via virtual HUD representation). Player max zoom in is 40 / 6 = 6 meters (600% zoom in).

Region System 100m-10000m
Bigger than chunks, they're a collection of chunks. They also cast a "shadow background" - a larger vague shape of the collective region that could be considered "underground" or "within the bowels" of said region. If player starts to dig out chunks and goes deeper into the area and there are still chunks around them in their "tunnel" they've made, then a dynamic background will be generated and obscure farsight horizon vision. This "shadow background" may have a color and texture, but also has muted saturation and darker brigthness compared to actual solid chunks / terrain etc.

NOTE- Regions differ from Continents and Celestials in that while the "shadow background" of a celestial and continent vanish as regions come into view, regions actually stay visible long after chunks and even clumps are visible on ground level. Regions are effectively the defacto "real shape" of whatever they are part of, be it planet, building or ship, with chunks defining the actual terrain, while regions providing the "web/net/frame/pinboard" that they're attached to and part of.

Continent System 100m-10000km
Even larger still than regions, these encompass regions. Usually they are only visible when they're far away and are used to breakdown large celestials into continents, to better narrow down which regions to load / unload / ignore when a player is traveling a celestial. They eclipse a region with their own "shadow background" that would appear in the horizon if an entire region were to be destroyed or removed on a celestial.

NOTE- Above mentioned "shadow backgrounds" are somehow programmatically generated, thought I do not know how. Mainly I want it to be possible to gradually split a large celestial or make a really deep and large hole in them - where if you fall / go into it, you'd be underground and unable to see the farsight horizon, since the celestial eclipses the view in some way through this "shadow flanking background" thing.

NOTE- Continents and regions can be as small as the minimum size and not even fill that. If this is the case and their mass is too low for a major gravity effect, then they are not classified as a celestial, but an entity, which is not a major galactic object that would play a role in the large scale gravitational fields of the star system, galaxy or a super cluster. Thats the difference between an entity and a celestial. Still, entities do behave similarly to celestials in terms of visual appearance; first as a dot, then as a square or circle, albeit much closer to the edge of the farsight horizon than a celestial, which appear more closer to the center instead. Because they are larger in size.

Celestial System 100km-max width limit (1M km)
Usually only visible very far away in the horizon on a clear star sky. These appear as a circle at medium distances and as a dot at far distances.

LOD SYSTEM
==========
Level of distance / Mipmapping (MIP = latin "much in small space") is a principle by which the entire universe is shown. Everything exists and is alive, but only things close to the player are most detailed and prioritised. That does not mean that the rest of the universe is inert or inactive or unloaded, but just operational at a much slower and basic level of detail. Far away celestials in the horizon only render as a tiny circle, while those even further render as a dot. Those beyond visual range are still alive as an abstract datapoint. Visually and functionally, as a player approaches the surface area of a celestial - from deep space to ground level - first they see a dot, then a circle, then that circle gains texture and gains saturation and brightness while it grows in the background, then it's continents come in to view as primitive shapes (circle or square (maybe triangle too)) from which the player will be more able to determine what type of celestial it is (a planet, star or a megastructure or some kind that is not a round circle uniform shape generally.).

After this, as moving closer to one of the continents, its regions begin to become visible, also represented as collections of circles or squares (maybe triangles too) in various configurations and sizes depending on the celestial and its individual continents.

Then, approaching a region, chunks - the actual terrain starts to become visible within a certain range of distance in the horizon.

Closing in further, now things like main structs and sub structs come into view as generic primitive shapes (squares, circles, triangles)

Getting even closer, now general larger components of these main/sub structs start to appear.
NOTE- its worth noting that either main or substructs can become visible depending on how big they are. Its not main first, then sub after. Some subs can be bigger than mains, so its all based on actual size dimension on when they appear in the horizon. The difference between subs and mains is that subs can go inside of mains. (like a main struct could be a cargo carrier ship or hangar, while sub struct is a utility vehicle onboard said carrier / hangar.) Since subs being inside mains are by definition smaller than the main they are in (unless they're only partially inside of a smaller main), then visually it all still works out fine.

These ^ "components" are essentially major features of a struct that are attached to them as addons. For buildings, these can be doors, windows, gates, antennas, machines, weapons and other things. Each of these appear based on their size, so some features will appear before others.

Not yet at the ground level - visible just at the edge of the background farsight view, player can now see most objects almost fully detailed. Most major features and components are visible, along with texturing. Clumps may be visible at this level too.

NOTE (all the above things mentioned in the LOD/MIPMAP section here are all rendered in the farview-horizon; a dynamic view rendered in the background, allowing the player a vision past the traditional limits of 2D sideview perspective. Objects based on size slowly shrink the farther they are from the player - eventually vanishing at a single horizon in the center. Players can see up, down, left and right. All 360' degrees.)

Finally arriving to ground level - and in the actual present area - players can now see most things as intended with all details fully visible and the smallest of clumps also to be seen.

Past this point, player can also use virtual interfaces to inspect individual objects in more detail for things that are not visible on the default LOD level (the one where the player is currently present in the game).

SOLAR SPAWN & CYCLE
===================

Order of stuff (during universe generation):
1. Solar system size determined by its allocated diameter/size and total weight of material within the cloud or void.
2. Then that material is randomly assigned to create any number of stars or any types, any sizes or similar sizes according to min-max thresholds, limited by the max budget alloted for stars. 
3. More stars means less material for other non-star celestials like planets and gas giants and rings/belts.
4. If multiple stars, then if one is sufficiently bigger than other, then it takes the center. If multiple stars are within the range of similar sizes, then they form a pair/ring in the middle with a random min-max spacing (up to 10% max distance from center of star system). If the stars are not of equal size exactly, then either two things will happen eventually with the passage of time: if the other sun is small enough, it gets absorbed into the bigger one(s). If its not small enough, it will siphon material from its not big enough friend to grow to be the same size as them. Eventually there will be an equilibrium - equally sized stars that are stable.
5. The generated stars can be anything from a gas giant about to ignite in a giant cloud, to a dwarf in a lesser cloud, to a main sequence star of varying sizes and intensity (red - slow cool, yellow - cruise hotter, blue - fast burning ultra hot), to a red giant, red supergiant, white dwarf, black dwarf, planetary nebula, neutron star, pulsar, blackhole, supernova remnant. There can also be rogue configurations (more common in voids or edges of galaxies) with even more random configuration that normally wouldn't happen in a "ideal natural cycle" of a galaxy. Galaxies don't exist in a vacuum seperately, but instead recieve effects of their neighbours that can sometimes shuffle things around on a small or even large scale.
6. During actual gameplay timeline, the universe would strive for equilibrium and thus do operation that gradually make things more flat, halted and equal. On a longer timescale, stars would undergo natural transformations due to age. If all the gas and dust have been formed into stable celestials and the star doesn't get any more external input from the star system or cloud/void or galaxy, then it will do what a star would do in real life; burn up its fuel, change into a red giant and collapse into a white dwarf -> black dwarf or neutron star or blackhole or super nova remnant.
7. On rare occasions, various celestials (planets, stars, comets, asteroids, moons, clouds) might end up drifting into a star system or cloud or galaxy for whatever reasons and may shuffle the peaceful order of an otherwise naturally stable, changing the course of what future holds for that star system.
8. Anyway back to point 5. After that, any leftover material is randomly assigned between 1-32 (minus the amount of stars) of celestials, be it planets, gas giants, clouds, rings, belts, asteroid fields, comets, individual orbiting large asteroids, moons. Plus debris fields, derelict space stations or other ruins from ancient times with possible loot and artifacts.
9. During actual gameplay, things will form naturally over time if the conditions are right. In phases of just a cloud of dust and gas, there is some randomness where it will decide how to disperse those materials according to a dice throw(RNG), but past that things will gradually form into celestials that'll go through their cycles.
10. Both during universe generation and actual gameplay, the universe will use its powers of equilibrium to eliminate density overflows (too many celestials in one location) or to resolve optimal positions of celestials in a star system (like moving stars to correct positions and adjusting their sizes). These mechanics also apply to the star system itself, as well as the clouds/voids, the galaxies/voids holding them and so on.



SOLAR LIGHT and HABITABLE ZONES
===============================

In this 2D universe things a bit different. Its not like the real life 3D world - but something that is more optimized for a 2D world; doing things on 2D's terms instead of pretending to be 3D.

Celestials do not spin. They also don't orbit. They don't move either unless already moving or moved by something. If moving and nothing is keeping the momentum up, they will gradually slow down while inside galaxies - at a slow rate, enough for them to drift quite far before they actually stop and settle.

Stars do not project light constantly and they do not project it as a directional radiation. Instead, they pulse between hot/cold and light/dark cycles, gradually glowing brighter until a certain peak, then becoming dimmer until a certain low and then repeating the pattern infinitely.

At a certain distance, stars are usually way too far to be more than just a dot in the sky among other dots. So from the maximum effective distance distance to the sun to the sun itself, in gradual sectors of intensity/strenght, the star will illuminate the entire zone uniformly and omnidirectionally. Essentially for a planet in the so called "luminant zone", which in itself also may have a "habitable zone" not too close to the sun and not too far away from the sun, when its "day" aka when the sun is bright, the entire planet will have this light, even the side that is turned away from the star.

Its worth noting that the dark cycle is still emitting a faint light and some warmth that on earthlike planets, you still get nights with a starry sky and some warmth that can sustain life. A dark cycle of a star is not the same as the star not being there or being turned off or something. Instead its just a temporary dimmer state of the sun.

This light/dark cycle can be randon on different stars in different star system, causing cool situations like some stars having really long cycles, causing month long days or short cycles, causing 2 minute days.

# Resource Processing
AAL has the technological capability to utilize most if not all matter, mediums and energy in the universe to craft anything that is needed.

The caveat is that its always cheaper, faster and more efficient to use existing deposits of something, rather than to transmute into it from something else.

There are these categories of stuff in the universe:
.particles, energy, heat, baryons T1 (lightest)
.gasses, basic atoms (periodic table 0-20) T2
.liquids, intermediate atoms, solids T3
(periodic table 20-80)
.complex atoms, heavy solids, nuclides T4
(periodic table 80+)
.exotic matter, composites, alloys, weaves, hyper energy, extreme materials. T5 (heaviest)

The rule of thumb is that its essentially following the periodic table, with basic- and ultra-energies at their respective ends.

Transmuting matter to something is easier the closer its target matter - considering it's position in the periodic table - is, aka heavier / complex (T4-T5) converts to T3 better than into T1-T2. Atom 1 aka hydrogen converts easiest to atom 2, aka helium. The chain is like this: T1 + T2 + T3 + T4 + T5. 

T stands for Tier (level of advancement).
Heavier elements tend to be more rare.
All matter degrades to lighter elements, especially if they're an unstable isotope (radioactive / volatile).
Energy cost wise in transmutation, both light to heavy and heavy to light are same.
Most natural heavy materials are created in extreme conditions (high forces of heat, gravity, pressure, electricity, energy, rays and/or very long durations of reverse erosion / anti-entropy created by unusual conditions or anomalies). The rest are artificially forged by beings. Lesser materials in nature come from heavier matter via breaking / eroding / entropy / annihilation / radiation / outgassing.

Transmutation can happen chemically/organically (slow, cheap, simple) or energy/force wise (fast, expensive, complex).

Homeworlds may have the industry and systems for powerful fast large-scale transmutation, but new uncharted worlds need to rely on existing matter deposits with only minimal transmutation and even then, in gradual steps along the chain.

As a result, on new worlds mining / harvesting activities are quite similar to those today in 2020, where mineral deposits are searched, scanned, surveyed and utilized. The collected material is then brought to refineries and sent along the manufacturing chain. Very similar to Factorio.

On homeworlds, all materials are already fully extracted and are merely recycled, kept in reservoirs / stockpiles and converted / used when and where needed. Only heat management, pollution and side effect factors are a concern, otherwise its sustainable abundance for a very long ass time. Apart from sending material offworld, but new matter comes from space anyway - naturally or otherwise, so its not like matter depletion is going to be a problem ever either.

Simply all-devouring amoebas that would absorb everything if left unchecked. The reports brought back by many of the probes were very troubling. The latter type of mindless alien monster races were detected, but these were far worse than anything seen within the milkyway. Far larger amounts, more vicious, more spread out, more advanced, more diverse, more evolved and even more destructive, hostile and exploitative.

And it was not even just one group either, but many. Some are biological, other mechanical while even others were something in between, like biomechanical. At a glance, the origins of these different races would be possible to guess. Hyper-evolved alpha beasts, spacefaring parasites and viruses, rogue undead mindless cyborg armies, berserker type self replicating war robots gone awry, all-devouring flesh swarms, biomechanical superweapons without a master, automated death-force doom fleets, anomalous chaotic nightmare beings, infected / corrupted / converted ex-civilizations that made fell victim to a bad decision or an unfortunate event that turned an entire reasonable civilization into a brain-dead horde of space zombies, malfunctioning grey-goo and everything and anything in between that could be a mix of any of these things. In total, they outnumber the entire milkyway galaxy's forces by an exponential degree. 

Worst of all, they all show signs of roaming, expansion and exploitation, leaving a trail of doom wherever they go, often deeply infecting whatever planet, rock or celestial body they come accross as a candidate for another hive or for total uncontrollable and unsustainable exploitation of resources that leaves the area dead. # AAL Lore, Basics
(This document explains the basic established rules of the ingame universe in my games.)

Overall the tone of the game's universe is grounded/realistic and it tries to aim for a believable world, with some softer edges. While it is a grounded scifi universe, its not a hardcore ultra-realistic world, just believable enough for a non-expert layman person to be like "yeah, that could make sense to me on some level".

Technology wise, there is FTL, advanced power sources, megastructures, artificial intelligence, sentient machines, advanced automation and industry, highly developed society and social systems, full sustainability on a galactic scale, enourmous superweapons with planetary scale destructive potential, biological immortality, advanced biomods amd augments, impressive genetical and biological engineering. In some regards, its a soft utopia with a happy populace with a bright future and an optimistic outlook, but with occasional dangers and challenges that never truly offer 100% safety.

However, there is no teleportation in this universe and FLT works only in proportion to how far away they are from large sources of gravity, like planets or stars - slowing down to a crawl the closer a FTL capable vessel comes to one, being mostly useless below the high orbit of a the equivalent of a moon sized celestial object; forcing ships to use conventional thrust or other mobility technologies to move about near and on a planet's surface or its atmosphere. It also renders any FLT based planetary bombardment weapons completely null and void, as FTL acceleration uses entirely different laws of nature compared to regular physics momentum and acceleration in this ingame universe.

Magic and mana does not exist at all, not in the sense of a traditional fantasy sense. However, psychic abilities like telekinesis and similar extrasensory perception abilites do exist, but are exceedingly rare and require exceedingly expensive, complex and delicate connections to the world's astral realm, a parallel dimension that is intervowen with the physical world, being sort of a limbo space where souls stays when they're not occupying a living body in the physical world due to a recent death. The astral realm is like an neutral amorphous soup that exists everywhere at the same time and is the place where souls go after a living being dies and from which a soul or multiple souls are deposited into a new being that is born in the physical world. The astral realm is like the aloof brains of the universe that act as the will of the universe, but lacks any sort of actual intelligence, operating merely on instinct. Those with an exceedingly strong enough spiritual connection to nature/cosmos may be able to tap into this will of the universe to cause changes within the physical world, manifesting as various psychic abilities.

The structure of the game's greater universe is also unique; its both finite and infinite at the same time - if it were visualized as a simplified 2D plane, it would look like a regular round bowl with a big wide flat area in the middle and then curved edges around it. The center area is the universe proper and is mindbogglingly wide, contains many superclusters that contain staggering numbers of galaxies within. A big bang event is only a ultra-rare occurance that only happens in very extreme cases and even then only ever affects a smaller portion of the universe, meaning big bang events are just a function of the universe rather than how it started. Instead, the universe was never truly born from any particular event such as the big bang, instead it simply has always existed without a beginning and without end, it simply exists as a fact and law of nature. Somewhere in this universe the milkyway galaxy and the solar system exists, mirroring our own real life counterpart. From the edges of this flat area, surrounding it, begins a curve that extends to infinity, starting gentle and eventually becoming a steep vertical 90' angle after several thousand or million lightyears. This curved area is a called the "wilderness" where ejected material from the flat base universe may eventually end up and may drift within the wilderness, often simply going in like a skateboarder into a quarter-pipe ramp, reversing its momentum and drifting/flying back into the universe proper. While in the wilderness, the ejected material may possibly colliding with other material, it may become reshuffled or mutated by the innate storms of the wilderness that are endlessly more intense the deeper up the curve something manages to travel or end up temporarily dissappearing in an anomaly wormhole, only to be spit out somewhere in the universe proper if a spacetime rift is opened due to an extreme high energy event or an extreme void event where a portion of a universe became so barren of any material that it forces a space-time rift to eject new material into it from the wilderness. Just to clarify, I'm using the 2D plane and bowl as a simplified metaphor, when the universe is 3D, and the bowl is more of a sphere with an inner stable zone and an outer wilderness zone that pushes ejected things back into the stable zone. Its a fully equivalent exhange based world that endlessly recycles itself - no matter is ever created or destroyed, only changes in state. The stable universe is only stable in name ofcourse, stars still due their whole life cycle thing, physics still govern the movement of celestial bodies and so forth, the inner stable zone is always in flux, evolving, changing, being as a whole a living breathing universe.

# AAL Lore, Weapon Variety
(This document explains the variety of weapon tech that )


Inspired by starsector and aided lightly by DxM/AC6, maybe with a touch of Mass Effect 3, I want bring armor mechanics and weapon types that create a level of complexity to combat that can cause resistance or opportunities to exploit.

Weapons namely fall into two categories; ballistic and energy.

Ballistic contains solids, fragmentation, kinetics, blades, hammers, penetrating perforators, shedders, cutters and physical impacts.

Energy contains shockwaves, incineration, vaporization, electrocution, waves, explosions, blasts, spalling, burning, tearing and ripping.

As an optional component, subvariant or addon, emp effects can exist too in energy weapons.

There's also elemental effects that both of these main categories can be imbued with - fire, acid, shock, slag, freeze, gravity and such.

Some weapons can be hybrids, being both ballistic and energy in totality or partially.

Some elements may override some effects of a weapon types innate attributes.

--

The main idea is that there are types of armor, shields or materials that things are made of that make them vulnerable or resistant to certain types of weapons.

Ballistics are great against things that can shatter, lacerate, shred, crack, eviscerate, cut, cave in, deform.

Energy are great for things that can twist, melt, tear, rip, stretch, burn, spall, change form, resist/absorb electricity, vibrate, undergo molecular shifts, mutate.

Each unit, object, surface, machine, vehicle, prop, item... they are all made from various materials. Presence of a material in it's composition will make it more vulnerable to the corresponding weapon type. Depending on the ratio, exact materials, their arrangement/refinement, they will provide different ratings of resistance against one weapon type or another - possibly even total immunity if a weapon is weak enough and the armor is strong/resistant against it enough.

Because there's this duality of weapons, nothing is fully safe no matter how hardened it is. Most objects will gravitate towards a balance of both materials to make them resistant against both weapon types atleast to some degree.

This creates a world where there are armors rated to withstand either both weapons fairly well or heavily focus against one weapon. Each armor class tries to either resist or make itself proof against up to certain levels of power.

This creates the Armor/Defense value/stat/parameter.

Alongside it exists the Integrity/Health value that is impacted when armor is breached. Armor may exist as an additional health bar, absorbing all hits before integrity starts taking a hit or it may exist like a Doom style armor that reduces an amount of damage by a percentage, both degrading the armor value, but also letting the health/integrity also take damage as it comes.

If the armor is dense enough and an attack is weak enough, it may harmlessly ricochet off and away. While this doesn't cause neither HP nor armor damage, it does still chip away at another counter, counting up with enough hits to count for a single point of armor damage eventually. Kind of like erosion or degradation of armor quality.

Shields - energy shields - are a essentially an artificial armor created and held intact through active energy supply. They work the same as armor, but require a supply of energy and a supporting generator to keep it existing and cohesive. Armor is like a naturally built-in embedded feature that can exist on its own, but shields exist only through assistance. They have the benefit of regeneration, but may have the drawback of catastrophic collapse or dangerous surges.

Integrity Health vs Armor; the difference is that the composition of integrity only defines vulnerabilities, while in armor it defines a protective shell may ricochet, absorb or mitigate that type of damage that leaks into the hull/integrity.

Ammo Types:

Below is an idea for a composite ammo system where instead of complete firearm rounds, these scifi weapons - called Firing Frames - use a system of internally manufactured ammo inside the gun itself. The ammo itself is usually a slurry, powder or aggregate that the weapon will extract a correct amount based on the type of projectile its programmed to launch, create that round either in advance or during the firing process and then launch, accelerate or eject said projectile out of the weapons barrel or whatever other ejection port type it has.

-advanced/complex projectiles commonly use more than 1 unit of ammo at a time.
-larger projectiles commonly use more than 1 unit of ammo at a time.
-ammo pickups come in small 8px and large 16px container formats.
-small gives 20 units, large gives 200 units.
-they're common to find and can be found in bundles.
-large ones are slightly rarer, but only by a factor of 1/10.
-all containers look the same, but have 2 colors to distinguish them.
-first color is predominant, while the second color denotes a subtype.
-ex, kinetic and shell are similar, so both are yellow, but have red and blue sub colors respectively to distinguish them.
-any ammo is superweapon capable, weapon simply has other drawbacks (weight, rof, ammo use, delays, cooldowns, inaccuracy, dangerous, etc).
-weapons may combine multiple ammo types to create special payloads.

AAL Mecha Types
===========


Types are almost like tiers; higher numbers have all the tech from lower numbers and are thus almost always superior by a vast margin.

Some units of lesser types may be highly specialised that they can outperform a higher type mecha in one or two aspects, but the higher types will still be stronger as a whole in every other capacity.

Each unit in any type can vastly vary in size, though types 3-4 assume at least a Gundam sized machine, roughly plus or minus some meters in any direction. Type 5 assume Evangelion/godzilla sizes more or less with no ceiling on how big it can get. Types 1 or 2 can be practically any size, from small to massive, with mostly their tech level, lack of complexity, design flexibility limitations, materials used, reduced features and structure keeping them from being greater types.



Type 1 - primitive
Slow lumbering machines with limited flexibility, mostly brute force style design.
Ex. Mechwarrior, AT-AT, APU, lost planet 3 drill mech, junk mechs, early mechs or pilotable robots.



Type 2 - advanced specialisation
Next generation units with overall more advancements compared to type 1, may also specialise in specific trait or doctrine, excelling in it, vastly outperforming a type 1.
Ex. Lost planet NEVEC mechs, muvluv alt, front mission, intrusion 2, metal slug 1-6, gunhed, macross grunts, gunarms, votoms, gunmetal.


Type 3 - large full aspect humanoid
Gundam sized on average, vastly uses better tech, design, materials, features and potential compared to any type 2 overall. Quite often fully humanoid, though alternative limbs are fine (spider legs, tracks, wheel legs, thrusters, tentacles, many arms/legs instead of just 2 each, head part of torso, etc.). Can be non humanoid too (zoids, animal, dino, insect, fish, bird, dragon, other).

Most crucially however, they feature humanlike manipulator arms with opposable thumbs, allowing for vastly adaptive combat abilities to switch armaments on the fly in direct battle. Type 1 & 2s don't have these commonly, more relying on mounted, pre-installed weapons and systems, perhaps at most having claws or rudimentary manipulator arms, which both are relatively basic compared to a type 3s arm/hand logistics / control interface system.

Though while they can specialise in a specific trait or doctrine, more often they are far balanced armor /weapons /mobility /sensor (AWMS) wise overall and capable of being a one unit army/operative that can survive independently, only needing a base or carrier for repairs. Most of AWMS of a type 3 are significantly stronger compared to any type 2, allowing them to easily overpower them in more than one way.

Fuel is also less of a concern due to own power plant/reactor system, extending operation time to far more than usually ever necessary.

They are the peak of conventional battle vehicles/mechas that use still-relatable modern-ish technologies. Anything beyond them is likely to be miracle-tech, because type 3s are still limited, needing to accompany other transport vehicles to take them somewhere far, be it in land, sea or space. And they still need bases for full repairs and maintenance checks.
Ex. Gundam/mobile suits/mobile armor, lost planet ptx160 hardballer, early armored core.


Type 4 - large beyond limits hyper machines
Similar to how type 3 compares to a type 2 - being better at everything overall - the type 4 does the same to type 3s, but stays a similar size. They're almost like super robots or future-tech Gundam, featuring materials, design, technology and systems that vastly outperform any type 3 by a huge margin.

They can fully repair, refuel and maintain themselves, not requiring bases or carriers.

They can also travel vast distances on their own usually, not needing to return to a base necessarily, but even keep going like a von Neumann probe (self replicating spacecraft robot) or just a solo nomad that can keep going virtually forever, only limited by the pilot.

They may have the capacity to even build additional machines, virtual intelligence operated battle or support units that can be commanded as a fleet by the type 4, effectively either spawning an army, seeding a base/city in a new remote location, gathering resources en masse from a deposit, deploying satellite coverage over a planet(oid) or creating deep space outposts/buoys for itself or allies to use.

Direct combat wise, the tech involved is super advanced.

Armor wise their internal structure may be so advanced that it rivals biological complexity and integration, featuring less moving parts that can brake down in favour of solid multilayer megastructure construction that is almost like a weave of armor and utilities, incredibly durable and integrated, potentially modular even.

They may even have energy shields, force fields or other exotic miracle tech defenses.

Weapon/sensors systems may also be more automated, versatile and assisted overall - besides being absurdly more powerful too.

# AAL Lore, Chaos System
(This document explains what the Chaos System is in my games.)

Below is an excerpt taken from the 'AAL: Superweapon' game's lore:

...the game's story takes place at this time, at the area of one such war laboratory complex that spans two star systems, one as the stable manufacturing and development complex spread over multiple planets, stations and colonies, while another one as a remote testing firezone with numerous mostly desolate, barren planets populated by the occasional outpost and orbiting monitoring space stations.

In particular, the war laboratories is loosely based on my old mod for Cortex Command that added a whole new faction to the game, named Alteisen Arsenal Laboratories (AAL for short), bringing powerful yet exotic new weapons, objects, items, spacecraft and units to the game. As I had grown as a person over the years, so has this faction, which originally was designed ad-hoc or on the go, with whatever seemed like a cool idea at first with little regard if it made sense in a real life context. Now its more organized and plans ahead, with many of the technologies given more thought in advance before actually making them.

The central event that kicks off the game is an experimental prototype planet destroying satellite cannon that is being tested for the time to blow up one of the desolate planets in the firezone star system. I'm still debating what the cause of the event that will happen as it fires (univeral law of nature or freak accident or anomalous material), but nonetheless something very unexpected happens when firing the weapon causes a dimensional failure, creating a gigantic tear / bubble / explosion / field / miasma / chaotic corruption that rapidly envelops entire firezone star system and also spreads far enough to reach the other manufacturing and development laboratory star system too, prompting an evacuation of all personnel outside the field that has now thankfully stopped growing and seems to be calming down, showing no signs of expanding further. Its like a giant wound in space-time had been created, and the surrounding area was completely saturated with the universe's "cosmic dimensional blood" that is now nullifying any further high intensity activity by the way of dangerous storms that destroy anything too large or numerous in the area for the time being as the universe slowly repairs itself.

The Chaos Management System:

The truth is that what happened, was for the first time ever, a weapon so ridiculously powerful was fired that it broke the limit of how much action the universe can handle at a time, causing it to reveal one of its more fundamental, severe countermeasures to heal the damage caused by the overkill caused by the superweapon. Its as if the weapon was so absurdly powerful and incorrectly configured that it caused a massive wound in the fabric of space-time itself.

This is a thing called the "chaos management system" that is unique to this particular (game) universe. Also called "Chaos System" for short. While "Harmony System" would be a more accurate name for it due to what its true purpose is, the fact that it bring about harmony through violent chaotic reactions and storm-like events makes the "Chaos System" feel more appropriate from the observer's point of view.

Commonly in video games in real life, I've discovered as a player that some games were designed with the mentality of no restrictions, assuming that technology would improve in the future infinitely and thus something that is laggy today, would be smooth on newer, more advanced hardware. From my experience this is a terrible idea and should not be done. In Cortex Command, the game was only ever really able to support a very small scale type of gameplay with a small number of objects that can exist on the level at any given time. Sadly, the developers (and by extention, the modders too) created gameplay and scenarios where these limits were doomed to be crossed, if the game was to be played seriously to any degree. 

Players would automatically end up in situations where their frames per second were down incredibly low numbers, below 10 fps and sometimes even less than 1 fps, turning the whole experience into a frustrating slideshow. As a game developer, I've been seeking to find a way to create a platform-- a universe where one could have all manner of amazing epic things happen fluently without it lagging all to hell. 

The solution was is what I named the "Chaos Management System", which in a nutshell essentially intervenes with the simulation by implementing certain clear consequences when the game is approaching or find itself in a situation that is beyond the means that the computer's hardware is capable of handling. The different actions range from subtle ones like unloading unimportant objects into the background, miniziming complex objects into simpler ones, grouping similar individual objects into a group entity, slowing down advanced processes to take more time like AI decisions and other similar opportunities like this. One of the more severe consequences are the shattering of space-time itself, causing a nullifying expanding void rift to appear and devour nearby objects that are causing the excess violation of spawning too much stuff or otherwise overloading the system beyond acceptable limits in order to preserve fps. This void essentially does not delete anything, but it may displace it, trap it, mutate it, modify it, transform it and make it reappear at a later undetermined time somewhere else, usually at the edge of the universe in a specialized "wilderness" area, a concept taken from the classic Runescape online game.

Returning back to the game's events, the superweapon was improperly configured and spawned too many particles at once or used a needlessly expensive algorithm to create its blast. Because of its enourmous size and the enourmous energies involved, the resulting chaos rift ended up being so large that it enveloped both star systems.

The superweapon causes a huge mess, leaving two star system enveloped in this strange miasma / field / cloud left by the chaos rift event and now the lab personnel would like to go back, clean up the mess, salvage what is still intact, remove any sources of danger, gather information on what the heck just happened, rescue anyone trapped within, resolve any dangerous crisis situations that may occur from erratic foreign beings having access to superweapon prototypes and rebuild the facilities so that the work can continue.

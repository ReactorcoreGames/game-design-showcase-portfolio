========
Closest to a local star


Note: each star system and its individual celestials may receive varying density of defenses and infrastructure. Less activity = less dense defences. Some star systems are also more engineered than others, while others are more natural and untouched like our solar system.

At minimum, for the most remote isolated areas, each celestial has at least 6 satellites and 3 ground stations. (orbital stations if its a gas giant or planet is otherwise too hostile to land on). There is also a hyperwave buoy and 20 automated drone guardian ships (two per each location), plus additional scout, maintenance, security, monitor, survey drone units.
Each location houses an empty visitor space for a 100 humans, should anyone get stranded in the area.

Star colonisation density, rate or existence depends on star's properties (stability, type, hazards).

Most worlds in milkyway are discovered, known, but not all are visited and much less have been colonised. Full colony saturation is still underway.

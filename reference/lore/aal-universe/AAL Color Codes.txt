Color coding:
Each branch of the civilization's vehicles, machines, objects, structures, items, uniforms, clothing, markings and systems would have a unique universal 2-3 color code (and any optional pattern) to identify the role / purpose / branch a thing belongs to.

First, a primary color(s) denote the general branch of operation and theme. 80%

Then, a secondary color denotes the specific sub category of this branch. 20%

Thirdly, an ornamental pattern, shape or texture denotes the specific object type of that sub-branch.

Lastly, a logo, insignia, name, numbering, id, suit patch and flag communicate the full description of the branch.

All are as stylish is possible, all are respected equally and all are different, yet cooperative and cohesive.

Full color of the rainbow is used. Attempt is made to give some level of relatability for quicker recognition.

Nature of colors themselves is taken into consideration when assigning them to purposes; muted natural colors are used in more general purposes, while garish, unnatural colors are used for when visibility matters or when attention is evoked.

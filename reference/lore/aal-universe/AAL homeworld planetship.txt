Homeworlds get at least 6 good gigantic mega thrusters built in each quadrant, first to simply move it to the habzone, though past that, they can be used to fly the planet like an enormous spaceship.

Granted, if the planet is an open top regular Earth like world, then more is needed to do so safely without breaking the environment.

For airless dome, tunnel, inverted earth, hollow colonies, enclosed fortresses, this is easier since they are already capable of being outside of the habzone.

For planets with an atmosphere, a protective outer shell extended from the orbital rings is installed. It contains the atmosphere, providing light, warmth, shielding, day/night cycles, seasons, collects higgs boson energy en masse and manages global cooling via external space contact.

In this way, a planet can use its thrusters to gently move about the universe, dodge threats, maintain its ideal conditions and relocate to another location to hang around in.

Since the sun is no longer the main energy provider, higgs boson energy takes its place as a replacement. The further from a sun, the better HB energy.

FTL is not possible for the planet itself due to the delicate balance it maintains. Maybe some sort of transport gravity cradle bubble harness gigantic exoship could make it doable, but not within the planet itself.

Unlike atmosphere planets, other airless celestials, stations, ships and megastructures, they are more easier to fly and even go FTL due to their more static format.

As an uncharted new planet is being converted to a homeworld class celestial, it is seen more as a spaceship earth as Buckminster Fuller termed it.

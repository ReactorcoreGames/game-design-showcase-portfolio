# AAL Lore, Space Monsters
(A lore document explaining what space monsters are in the AAL Universe.)

In the far future where mankind has achieved world peace on earth/solar system, developed FTL and other advanced hi-tech in all fields of science to allow for permanent colonization and exploration of space.

Humanity first expanded to colonize the solar system, sent probes to discover info on surroundings, then kept going beyond the solar system far into outer space, colonizing and exploring the entire milkyway galaxy bit by bit, all the while improving /building /expanding /advancing its existing assets with megastructures, better infrastructure, bigger colonies, outposts, space fleets, defences, science facilities/hubs, industry, agriculture and automation.

Through out the milkyway, alien races were met, most of which could be sorted into two categories;
1. Sentient reasonable neighbours that could always eventually be persuaded to work together for the benefit of all, no matter how wierd they were compared to humans.
2. Beast-like rabid monsters that communication was completely impossible with due to their make up, physiology, design. Mindless rogue animals that were concerned with only instinctual or pre-coded behaviours, due to lack of sentience, awareness, intelligence and comprehension of any kind. Simply all-devouring amoebas that would absorb everything if left unchecked. To these, everything that is not them is a bacteria or food or trash to be disposed off, while uncontrollably multiplying and spreading everywhere with no regard or awareness for anything around them.

With the former group, almost no wars occured at all. If they did, they were short and usually resulted from a misunderstanding, fear or stupidity. Past that, conflicts were always possible to solve once proper communication and understanding between the aliens was established. From there, it was always easier and faster with each newly encountered alien race thanks to advances in better technology, services and products developed by humanity (and with joint effort from other allied alien races, as time went on). Eventually, all alien races along with humanity became unified as a single galactic combined race. With medical science being advanced enough to easily modify bodies from one form into another as well as mechanical and biomechanical cyborgization being possible (partially or even fully), eventually it didn't really matter what race one was, since they could become anything at any time. The final result was a soup of all sorts of personalized beings that didn't really belong to any race, but could take inspiration and mix anything they wanted, whatever suited their fancy, environment, culture and style. Also immortality was achieved for all. Yay.

With the latter group however, war was common, though probably the monsters didn't recognize what the concept of war of really was, being the mindless amalgamation they are. Still, due to lack of intelligence and sentience, the only benefits they had were limited to massive numbers, relentless tactics, fearlessness and no remorse. Besides that, they were predictable, still relatively contained and young within the milkyway galaxy, undeveloped and unprepared against a sentient, thinking race with a hi-tech military and advanced tactics and strategy. Most conflicts were one-sided and quickly over for the benefit of humanity and allied races.

In the end, the entire milkyway galaxy was fully conquered and colonized for the combined galactic race which the humans initiated originally.

However, the monstrocities encountered in the milkyway galaxy were only a tiny taste of whats to come beyond its borders, in the neighbouring galaxies and the dark space between them. Having had extensive experience with the local milkyway galaxy on what types of things are possible to encounter, humanity/combined galactic race, sought to investigate the space beyond the milkyway with the use of automated probe ships. The reports brought back by many of the probes were very troubling. The latter type of mindless alien monster races were detected, but these were far worse than anything seen within the milkyway. Far larger amounts, more vicious, more spread out, more advanced, more diverse, more evolved and even more destructive, hostile and exploitative.

And it was not even just one group either, but many. Some are biological, other mechanical while even others were something in between, like biomechanical. At a glance, the origins of these different races would be possible to guess. Hyper-evolved alpha beasts, spacefaring parasites and viruses, rogue undead mindless cyborg armies, berserker type self replicating war robots gone awry, all-devouring flesh swarms, biomechanical superweapons without a master, automated death-force doom fleets, anomalous chaotic nightmare beings, infected / corrupted / converted ex-civilizations that made fell victim to a bad decision or an unfortunate event that turned an entire reasonable civilization into a brain-dead horde of space zombies, malfunctioning grey-goo and everything and anything in between that could be a mix of any of these things. In total, they outnumber the entire milkyway galaxy's forces by an exponential degree. 

Worst of all, they all show signs of roaming, expansion and exploitation, leaving a trail of doom wherever they go, often deeply infecting whatever planet, rock or celestial body they come accross as a candidate for another hive or for total uncontrollable and unsustainable exploitation of resources that leaves the area dead. They are all like a horrific plague sweeping accross the galaxy, killing everything in their way. There have also been a few incidents at the borders of the milkyway galaxy of meeting small detachments of various monster fleets, resulting in damages and casualties from the battles to destroy these wandering scraps that had come to the milkyway by chance.

Horrified by the discovery and alarmed by the eventuality of what would happen if a larger detachment would arrive to the milkyway, the combined milkyway race began preparations and programs to do something about this situation. War laboratories were expanded and new facilities were designed and built, with full authorization to create the most powerful superweapons that previously had no real reason to exist.

At this time, there is no rush. The military of the combined galactic race is formidable and the defences are secure. The random stragglers that venture into the area can be defeated with some effort, though usually some damages and casualties occur. Even then these wandering scraps of outside monster fleets that by chance happen to come to the milkyway are very rare, small in number and irregular. They don't come often and they don't come intently or prepared. Meanwhile, the distant megafleets and large concentrations of monsters are extremely far away and busy fighting one another to really care about invading the far away milkyway at this time. There are certainly decades, centuries maybe even a few milennia of time for the milkyway residents to safely develop the technologies and superweapons needed to respond to the eventual dangers when the various monster groups will decide to invade the milkyway.

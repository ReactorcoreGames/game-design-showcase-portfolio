Placement of facilities and infrastructure:

Note: planets may have only few specific areas that are habitable / worth using. Everything else may be unavailable forever or until phase 2 rolls around.

Note2: some planets may have no atmosphere, lesser / extreme gravity, strong pressure, extreme temperatures, hostile flora / fauna, hazards (local or space), nasty weather (sideways glass shard rain at mach 5), special conditions, different resources or it may be a gas / liquid giant planet that can only be habitated in orbit. These require special considerations for habitats and planet utilization.

Note3: remember the NIMBY principle (not in my back yard) for placing cities, doing zoning and avoiding poor social design that would make life less wonderful for people.

Note4: variety is key. Phase 1 resembles opportunistic microbial spreading development quite alike on Earth, with expansion done where most convenient, easiest and cheapest. Every new colonized world will be different due to this, but variety will still be present during phase 2-3, since zoning can be done in infinite ways on the local micro scale.

Note5: for cities, megacities, arcologies, cityblocks(dredd), large colony interiors, huge space station layouts, either adopt the venus project circle node setup or cruise ship deck setup. Not applicable to smaller compact habitats.

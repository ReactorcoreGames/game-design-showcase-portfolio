# AAL Lore, Milky Way Civilization
(This document explains what the joint friendly human-alien civilization of the milkyway galaxy is like overall.)
(Note: both original humans species and any encountered friendly intelligent aliens species all ultimately assimilate to become the biologically immortal and highly customizable omni-species called homo mugen as it is simply too beneficial for neither species not to.)

## Some tidbits about milkyway civilization in this game's ingame universe:
(NOTE: All the below info is applicable only after the events of 'AAL: Eradication Wars', prior to it was more akin to our modern IRL world in 2018 in regards to societal norms, albeit with more advanced technology like interstellar spaceflight and mechas. Most of my projects take place after 'AAL: Eradication Wars' unless stated otherwise.)

- No parenting, no families, no relatives. None of these hold any value. At some point in the distant past everyone became aware how unfit anyone is for parenting, so the brightest minds created a better system for newly born humans where they're raised in healthy environments of abundance, learning, curiosity, independence, safety, honesty, love, empathy, collaboration and unity, without being shy about talking about the more rough things about the world, like death, extinction, violence, human history of stupidity, fragility of environments people flora fauna ecosystems relationships, seeing the world from other's perspectives, effects of inequality and respecting power and being aware of responsibilities. Any being from any race that was not brought up by this system from birth will be given ample reason and evidence to support for them to come willingly into re-education facilities where they'll be detained until full certainty has been assessed that they are suitable to join the rest of the galactic community. Plenty of effort will be done to do this in a way where the person transforms from within before the use of force is necessary to convert them. If it is apparent that an individual is hard-coded to disagree with the doctrine of the combined galactic community, they will be made to reset and reborn by force, otherwise there'd be an individual with access to items equivalent of nuclear weapons quite freely to cause horrific mayhem upon others. That said, if a being is sentient and capable of though, feeling, emotion, logic, regardless of how alien they are, they are 100% likely to see the offered world as worth subscibing to.

- Minimal personal belongings. There simply is minimal need for such things when everything is available anywhere and is available in abundance. Anything one can think of as a personal posession can be more or less rendered obsolete. Keys? biometrics or already sewn to whatever clothes they have. PDA/smartphone? The part that matters is data and preferences, just download it from a cloud and you're back in business, no matter where you got the device from. Wallet/ID? part of a universal registry system that is present through out the entire galaxy. Shelter, Food, Water, Electricity, Utilities, Hygiene, Entertainment, Tools, Transport? Everything is free, offers privacy, is clean, sanitary and quality is guaranteed. Clothes? Most clothes have self cleaning features, customization features to change colors, shape, lenght and patterns and just like any item they are available for free to be dispensed and returned whenever wherever as well as coming in many varieties. Speaking of variety, anything can be customized via computer and custom printed as a preset, whether it be clothes, dishes, tools, terminals, phones... anything. Recycling and waste disposal is handled automatically as people use items, wear them down and return them back. Since there is no arbitrary indoctrination for endless consumption or need to amass anything due to everything being abundantly available, it is both sustainable and efficient on a galactic level. Thanks to a high level of empathy, healthiness, welfare and education, a universal standardized galaxy wide secure decentralized network through which everyone that is part of the combined galactic race is registered to is something that people can actually trust fully as an infallible guardian and keeper of belongings/ data of an individual citizen, even in the event of extreme disaster.

- Immortality from age, modifying ones body, curing any illnesses and becoming digital to becoming human back again is all easy, commonplace. People can easily access medical stations to change themselves to anything that is biologically feasible, treat any defects, illnesses and traumas painlessly. People can still die if killed, thought if data backups exist of the original spine and brain, the person can be revived with a new body. If such data is not present and the person dies, they're permanently gone. Speaking of modifying oneself again, as long as it is biomedically, cybernetically or virtually feasible and not unsustainable, dangerous or harmful to surrounding environment or other population and not requiring abnormal intake of any irregular food, then the person can do and be anything they like. Change the color of skin, eye, hair, nail, teeth, blood, or flesh? done. Become a furry? done. Grow a tail? done. Become tall or short? done and done. Grow a pair of wings? no problem. Want two dicks instead of nipples? go ahead. Become a futanari? sure. Snake tongue? cool.
As long as its nothing stupid, dangerous, extreme or literally impossible to biologically achieve, unsustainable for longterm living, or incompatible with the rest of civilization in regards to accessibility, then its possible and doable.

- No rituals, no superstition, no ceremonies, no nonsense. Thanks to an ever present AI guardian that assists all citizens in any task, even providing social, sexual, love, empathy needs, the AI will always be there to give people known information about a certain subject that is (mostly) unbiased, neutral, objective and factual. People can instead just enjoy life without having to devolve into retardation or dumb shit.

- No politics or politicians. No laws. Its a natural law resource based economy. People are educated, everything is automated, information is transparent, universal standards are implement everywhere for maximum quality and compatibility for everyone and experts are the ones in charge of the fields they're specialized in, assisted by integrated networked AI support, collaborating and communicating with those departments that are affected by their work. Everything just works. And even when it breaks, it gets fixed and people are happy. Leaders do exist, but to clarify they exist in the sense as pioneers that spearhead and mastermind operations on the front being part of the effort that others follow their example, not as commanders that throw orders at their subordinates.

- Crime has been eliminated entirely and reframed as a sign of a person needing support, empathy, education, defusal, restraint and attendance, to be heard. Instead of a punitive system or the concept of good/evil, nor the many concepts of right/wrong, good/bad, praise & reward, punishment and guilt, etc. ...instead of all that, there is a restorative based justice system for correcting behaviour to help people make life more wonderful and serve life, reconnecting them with their true needs and feelings via Nonviolent Communication by Marshall B. Rosenberg.

## Infrastructure and Transit
A world of future that is designed for a positive well-being human habitation and operations, regardless of sector, be it industrial, residential, commerce, maintenance, waste disposal, farming, education, medical, military, transit networks, spaceports, docks, space colonies, remote outposts or even FTL starship premises. The different main sectors are color coded with a distinct color, while also identifiable via secondary accent color. Spaces are clean, organized, standardized, designed with purpose yet enjoyable to be in, appear lived in and full of tiny details that show activity of peaceful prosperous thriving human life. 

Even places like maintenance access zones or industrial places aim to create safe and stylish working conditions, even if its all based on simple mass-produced prefab building techniques. There are some elements of greenery where appropriate but its not a fusion of nature and human; natural preserves and parks exist harmoniously alongside human infrastructure and settlements, maintaining a healthy balance between the artificial and natural, keeping the two mostly separate with easy access in both ways. 

Transportation is based on sensible solutions like trains, mass transit and minor pedestrian vehicles (bicycles/scooters/rollerblades/skateboards, but also hybrids between a car and those small vehicles that would still fit into narrow spaces.) Most of cities and urban spaces are walkable with lesser emphasis on vehicles like a car or larger things like trucks. Although its the future, its not an absurd one where flying cars dominate the skies as the personal method of transport for every citizen, no; instead, energy efficiency and safe design is still going to favor most modes of transportation that stick to the ground and use wheels, tracks, hover cushions and more sensible options.

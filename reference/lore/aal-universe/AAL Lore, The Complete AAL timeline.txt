# AAL Lore, The Complete AAL timeline
(This document explains the entire greater timeline of periods/eras/events of the unified persistent ingame universe in which most of my games take place in.)

This contains a general timeline of most of my ingame universes. There are no specific year numbers or dates, just periods of time where a specific main thing is ongoing. They're all in sequence. Originally I made this document with this idea in mind:

"If AI can produce an entire tv show series based on a prompt, this would be what I wanna do: (all below entries are effectively separate TV shows or Movies)"

I'm calling it the AAL timeline as it has a recurring military technology company making a cameo in most of the series. AAL stands of Alteisen Arsenal Laboratories, a corporation similar to Anaheim Electronics from the Gundam Universal Century universe that remained a neutral entity that supplied all sides with the same weapons, simply because they were good, reliably and well designed. Kind of like IKEA but for all kinds of military, industrial, power and aerospace tech instead of furniture. Its also a slight nod to TOHA/TOA Heavy Industries from Tsutomu Nihei's manga works.


## Timeline

### IRL 2019 Counterpart and Early Space Exploitation
+ 2019 real world is sort of the baseline from which this ingame universe begins and diverges from. Humanity slowly begins to expand within the solar system, making use of the vast resources found and begin building the first ever basic megastructures.

### The Old Mentality World
+ With Solar system being colonized for the most part, humanity continues now past its borders to colonize and explore surrounding star systems. More megastructures are built, more advanced and bigger than before. Sentient AIs (synths) are created by accident that cause a stir in the world, eventually coming to terms with having robots live among humans, in addition to non-sentient AIs still being used for automation, labor and caretaking roles. Both humans and synths notice that neither is perfect and they both rely on each other to live a better life. Synth may be better at some things, but their capabilities highly rely on what body and components they have - with the higher end ones being exceedingly expensive and require rarer materials that are harder to replace, compared to the carbon based human body that has evolved over thousands of years to live and evolve effortlessly on earth, using abundant nutrients provided by nature. Meanwhile, first signs of alien intelligence is detected, but not yet encountered.

### A Dividing World
+ With growing number of human colonies across a few star systems, they begin making first contact with various alien species - some primitive, some intelligent, some are just mere animals. Encounters lead to many discoveries in science and they plant the first seeds that will eventually change the fate of humanity forever as they discover new things about the science of biology and genetics from multiple sampled alien dna that could allow them to develop a genetic transformation into a new superior upgrade for the homo sapiens species: the homo mugen. Meanwhile, tensions between human colonies begin to grow, due to ideological difference, petty reasons, the classic seven deadly sins, misunderstandings, stupidity, and bigotry. Frustrated scientists, philosophers and leaders from both certain human factions and the newly discovered alien races that finally offer an outsider's perspective on the faults of humanity, see that the same old ways of thinking and doing things that humanity has been infamous for is really bad for future existence of humanity and the other alien races, so they want to create a complete transformation of humanity and intelligent alien that would not only achieve true peace and harmony, but make it permanent, natural and self-sustaining. A plan is set in motion to transform both the human race and alien race(s) into homo mugen.

### Eradication Wars
+ An action show with multiple episodes that take place in the eradication wars era. Lots of battle, combat, annihilation. Exceptionally breathtaking and brutal. Setting is where humanity that has begun colonization of space beyond the solar system, creating a world similar to Legend of Galactic Heroes in terms of scale. Conflict is between two mentalities that have reached a point of no return, no reconciliation, no hope, no mercy and no remorse. The mentalities are domination-submission scarcity-enforcement hierarchy-roles driven people and collaboration-cooperation boundless-abundance anarchy-freedom driven people. The final straw is when the introduction of Homo Mugen is introduced, a genetically invented new species of mankind that can assimilate/transform regular Homo Sapiens into its own kind and this begins to spread amongst colonies in a silent 'Plague Inc' game type of ways (except instead of a deadly virus spreading, its more like those happy positive meme playthroughs that turns everyone into a furry or monstergirl). A military build up occurs on both sides, conflict and hate rapidly escalates over policy of newly created technology to finally gain biological immortality (only against aging, not becoming invulnerable) and eventually both sides understand that the universe is too small for them to coexist, leading to the Eradication Wars, where only one side will remain, while the other side are annihilated to extinction. Space battles, superweapons, mechs, brutal planetary skirmishes... Pure hell and apocalypse without anyone holding back.

### Golden Age Expansion of Space Colonies
+ Post-ERA period of pre-galactic colonization. (Note: the latter side, Homo Mugen, won the war, all remaining Homo Sapiens converted to Homo Mugen species) Starts with discovery of even more diverse friendly or neutral alien races, but ends with discovery of the dreaded space monsters. About a better society driven by NVC, innovation, wellbeing, unlocking true digital immortality (making all Homo Mugen virtually invulnurable and unkillable forever), prosperity and a future world that spans many star systems with happiness. About finding solution where everyones needs are met and things like suffering, conflicts and hatred is finally eliminated to a point where it will never cause wars within civilizations. A true victory over the seven deadly sins.

### Fight for Milkyway Galaxy, Early Period
+ Space monster wars - early stages, the fight for milky way galaxy. Dormant or active monster are discovered, fought, defeated. Each episode is a new nightmare that is either different or worse than the last one, eventually leading to an epic final fight crossfire with multiple monster groups, humans and allied aliens all fighting eliminate each other. Humans and allied aliens remain, but as probes go out to explore outside the milky way, much bigger threats are discovered.

### Fight for Milkyway Galaxy, Late Period
+ Space monster wars - middle stages. Milky way is still underway of being colonized with many unexplored pockets still left uncovered, including the galactic core area. But humanity + allied aliens now have established themselves as the dominant force in the milky way. Problem is that epic level space monster threats are coming or drifting into the milky way that make the previous space monsters seem trivial by comparison. The galaxy spanning war gets intense as humanity fights as best as it can against the horrors of the cosmos. This series ends with humans defeating a major threat, but realizing that more are coming, the universe is in danger of being ravaged without control and attrition wise this isn't sustainable to fight defensively.

### Intergalactic Deep Space Expeditions
+ Maverick intergalactic agent xenoarcheologists - humanity needs new technology; new weapons to turn the tide to make the fight trivial and automated against the space monsters far in the universe as well as the ones that dare come towards the milky way Galaxy. The ability to shoot far, shoot hard and eradicate sustainably, making the universe safer. Human sciences and milky way resources are limited, so agents are sent out in stealth ftl ships to explore the universe and find ancient alien tech to bring back to milky way to solve this problem. Sources of space monsters are uncovered, useless tech is found, cautionary tales of past civilizations are seen, unstable dangerous tech that ends up being destroyed, controversial tech is discussed with the council, components of the needed tech are found and finally the good stuff is acquired. An epic return trip is made to bring the physical components back (info was already sent via hyperwave) then humanity constructs an epic superweapon and tries to fire it. Cliffhanger ending.

### Defence of the Milkyway Galaxy
+ Events of AAL superweapon occur as a dimensional reality distortion fault occurs, plunging an entire group of star systems into chaos. Strange alien dimensional monsters are encountered, fought, destroyed and cleansed, reality is fixed and the problems of the superweapon design are fixed. A new superweapon is built and fired just in time to successfully obliterate an incoming megafleet of space monsters. The milky way galaxy is no longer on the back foot; we have now the tech, weapons and know-how to use them. Its now a matter to go on the offensive to rid the space monsters from this universe once and for all.

### Space Monster Annihilation Campaigns
+ Space monster wars - end stages. Events of Standalone unfold as autonomous berserker/von Neumann probe style superweapons are built. They are tested, improved and after a few accidents, finally perfected and sent into the universe to rid space monsters, save planets from being corrupted, clear out places that have been occupied, destroy planets that are lost, and create the infrastructure for a safe sustainable future world.

### Exploration of the Universe
+ Events of Planet Warfare*  begin. With the universe now a safer place and humanity now able to colonize outside the milky way, we begin to explore and colonize the rest of the universe. In this series, a random slice of life episode of a random protag per episode(s) is shown doing something in this wide open universe. The series ends with edges of the universe coming into view. Humanity discovers the "wilderness" and that the universe is both infinite and finite at the same time. (Note: Planet Warfare is a WIP game that has a misleading name, atleast by today context. It started off an open world space conquest game, but later turned into an all-aspect open world game where you can play as a free roaming person living within the universe and even visit the wilderness, solving its mysteries, solving hard problems with long term sustainability, occasional participate in wars that take place on planets or in space, do slice of life living. Kind of like microsoft's Freelancer game or Star Citizen/Elite Dangerous, but way more all-aspect, not just space-flight.)

### Exploration of the Wilderness
+ Continuation of planet warfare, focusing on the Wilderness - the outer borders of the universe, with characters venturing into it to find weird anomalies, understanding the structure of the wilderness and learning to live with it, utilize it and respect it. (Throw bad stuff in, get bad stuff out, galactic civilizations learn to become harmonious and organized to reduce the risks of the Wilderness).

### To Infinity and Beyond
+ The endless vibrant cycle of the universe. Sort of "conclusion to planet warfare" but not really. It shows that  despite conquering or mastering the universe, nature is still so vast and complex that the universe will never run out of surprises as places decay, reinvent, reshuffle and randomize. Old places become unrecognizable, people change, nothing stays the same except for the laws of the universe. Each day is exciting and the universe is incapable of ever becoming boring, old, or dying. Humanity learns to accept this infinity and embraces it with happiness.

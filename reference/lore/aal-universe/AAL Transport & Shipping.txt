Accessibility; can pickup invalid itself into cabin, even if its just a spine and brain, or only upper torso left of poor person. AI can deduct an emergency, administer first aid, autodrive vehicle to medical facility by itself.
Refuel, rearm, repair; vehicle is designed that its ports for input & output, maintenance access and shape is compatible and standardized with the appropriate vehicle bay that will be able to easily and quickly service it.
Manual vs AI autodrive; usually AI does all the driving while pilots simply tell it where to go and what to do. Even if manual control is initiated, usually its a simplified and streamlined interface similar to wasd keyboard + mouse set up or any other similar easy control operation that focuses on the "what" and not the "how".
Usability design; With the exception of recreational vehicles - like wingsuits, jetsuits, unicycles - most regular vehicle designs require minimal athelitisism, skill, health, strenght or soberness to operate, even manually. They often prefer stable movement and do less acrobatics, keeping users upright, comfortable, in control and safe.
Manual control systems; neural link aka mind, console, joystick, keyboard + mouse, integrated controller like built into gloves or sleeves or thighs, gestures, voice, body-as-controller, head only, vision only (blink + look), custom combination of any mentioned.
For civilian pedestrians, propulsion / locomotion / movement system are designed to be environment and surface safe, preferring magnets to hooks, arms to tapes, wraps to goo, suckers to glue in order to prevent damage, wear or dirtying surfaces.
Reuse vs disposable; to increase usability, recycle effectively and not leave waste to pollute or occupy the environment, vehicles are non-disposable default. Some vehicles may have one-way only design where they dock, transform, decouple, separate or convert to something else at their final destination. Though even then they can be still easily reclaimed and recycled.

Ideas for faction weaponry or doom mod protag weapons of Super Warrior (SW) units.

SW units are advanced elite hightech armored humanoid combatants that wield devastating weaponry capable of annihilating armies, cities, mountains, and fortresses at the top end, while on the lower end there are some weapons that belong more to tactical class and a even a few non-lethal ones.

The idea is a super soldier that uses suit-reactor or weapon built-in reactor powered energy weapons to effortlessly fight off hordes of enemies and easily take out heavier and larger foes too.

Weapons:
Name: Variable Atomic Rifle
Class: Annihilation. It's a city buster assault weapon that doesn't care about collateral damage. Has smart sensors to protect user from not blowing themselves up with the weapon, especially with the atomic blast firemode.
Appearance: vaguely resembles a dark carbon hedgehog with a glowing top section. Pointy barrel at the end, comfortable stock at the rear. Good ergonomics for both hip fire and aimed fire.
Function: can be configured to fire a nuclear beam that punches through, or a blaster shot that detonates with a large atomic blast, or a dragon shotgun that will create a wide shaped-charge like blast, shredding large areas, or a rapid fire pulverizer machine gun of meltashot style hits. Operates on a capacitor and charge up function. Low power hits are limited by capacitor recharge rate, while high power hits need both capacitor charge and a firing chamber build up.

Meltashot Pistol:
Class: Annihilation. A sidearm for when collateral damage isn't an issue. Can essentially shoot stuff through thick walls.
Appearance: a fairly standard mk23 ussocom (45 acp) sized relatively midsized pistol. Comes with a tactical module (light, laser, x-ray scanner, range finder), iron sights and comrtable pistol grip. Mildly futuristic.
Function: fires a searing blaster round that can punch/melt through a few dozen meters of solid ground, rock and metal. Leaves big fist sized holes, or rather tunnels, where the shots go through. Weapon can be tweaked to fire deeper but with faster capacitor depletion or more consistently without being bogged down by capacitor recharge, but less penetratively.

Wrist PDW:
Class: Tactical. The power of a 45 acp repeating pistol an infinite ammo energy weapon mounted on a wrist.
Appearance: forearm mounted, either part of a battlesuit or a standalone unit with a frontal barrel and a strap to secure it to the forearm snugly and comfortably. Side mounted heatsinks to dissipate heat.
Function: two firing modes; one is a simple blaster shot that punches hard and flies true, while the latter is a ultra rapid fire spreaded spray gun that can be used against soft numerous targets that are dispersed. Can be used for suppression too.  May also include a third lightning shock mode for non-lethal purposes or a 50k lumen blinding flash shot as another non-lethal non-kinetic stun/flinch attack.

Cold Photon Blade Hand Matrix Projector
Class: Tactical. A weapon that makes lightsabers look like a joke. A pseudo melee/short-range energy cutter thick blade weapon that can split into multiple thinner blades.
Appearance: looks like a hardened tech/combat glove with an extended section that wraps under the forearm. Usable as a weapon only if not holding any other firearms in that hand.
Function: uses an EM field to manifest a ghostly cold white blade of energy originating from on top of the users hand that can extend up to 7 meters, holding together as a single thicker solid blade or up to 20 (4 per each finger) thinner "wire" blades that can be spread apart like a fan. Has smart sensors to prevent user from chopping themselves unintentionally. Can also invert and project blades from the palm of the hand instead of top of hand or from fingers. Surprisingly intuitive to use. Can cut through average materials like wood and bone, especially in the single thick blade mode or palm mode. Less so in thin wires mode, which excels better with soft materials like flesh and cloth. Can do crazy stuff like curve the blades, form them into shapes and create "laser barriers".  The user thinks/wills it, the blade obliges.

Forearm Ion blade/blaster/shield:
A more powerful, bulkier and devastating melee weapon with an ranged option and a protective barrier shield. Not as flexible and exotic as the photon hand blade matrix, but still versatile to provide either a powered single blade or 3 spreaded fan blades, like a wolverine's claw (X-Men, not the animal). Can cut through rock and metal, extend up to 16 meters or launch an unstable blaster shot projectile upto a 100 meter away. It's not a firearm, but can be used as one in a pinch. It can also project a full body sized oval barrier shield to protect its user from incoming physical and energy based blows.

Buster Long Rifle
(Something more powerful than the VAR, has the ability to charge up an immensely powerful blast that can be considered a full-on tactical or even strategic super weapon. Maybe folds into the back and is shoulder mounted or hip mounted (think Daemon x Machina rail guns or beam cannons). Can have an extended scope/sensor package for beyond visual range targeting. Maybe has a split beam function for multitarget strikes or a curve beam ability. Maybe has a tachyon lance style mode.

Variable Thermal Energy Particle Rifle (TER)
Class: Tactical
While the VAR is used as a "shotgun" style weapon that fires a powerful salvo as a burst with a slower rate of fire, TER on the other hand is for versatile continuous fire operation instead.
It also comes with multiple fire modes:
- high power semiautomatic penetrating blaster shots
- medium power 1-15 second beam torrent (variable intensity settings, think prothean beam rifle)
- low power spray gun (variable spread setting; none, tight, wide, almost dome)
- ultralow power long range constant carving laser beam (anti drone point defence) with a sustained beam for 6 minutes.
For reference, high power shots easily penetrate 20m of rock, fire about 0.5 rof up to a range of 8km with about 50 rounds before needing to wait for a bit (3-5 secs) for the weapon to regenerate to full when not actively firing. Each shot creates a fist sized hole with an intense heat aura and a forceful kinetic impact.
For comparison, the beam penetrates 1m/s of rock with only 10% of the kinetic impact force during that 1 second operation time, but allows for sustained fire to "swipe" multiple targets for severe damage.
Low power pellets are more akin to a barrage of 45 acp bullets from a WW2 era Thompson SMG with similar damage profile per pellet.
Mounting options:
- as a standalone rifle instead of VAR
- as a combination dual VAR-TER weapon
- hip mounted gimballed auto gun (think VSBR, Freedom Gundam railguns, etc.)
- predator plasma caster style over shoulder auto turret
- deployable turret/drone

Grav-orb munition bomb dispenser/launcher
- indirect, bounce/ricochet, elemental payloads for area denial, or clearing spaces akin to HE/thermobaric grenades. Close range homing/smart/airburst capabilities to wiggle through winding corridors on their own to reach targets without needing a hard surface bounce angle or an immediate corner. Sort of like mini kamikaze drone bomblets.
- they're about the size of a marble, fairly low power compared to the other arsenal, dispensed from a launcher under the arm or from the torso or calf. Wherever is most convenient considering the rest of the arsenal. Limited ammo, reloadable with supply packs (sort of like using an EpiPen).

Common to all (non-mounted) weapons:
- micro thrusters to let weapon fly to user or where the user commands it to and remotely fire as long the weapon has capacitor charge or an onboard reactor.
- gravity tether to let user easily pull and recall their weapon if it's ever out of their hands, placing it safely back on their hands or holster somewhere on the armor.
- virtually infinite ammo either due to suit reactor power supply or onboard weapon reactor power supply.

Some weapons are supported by advanced features provided by the battlesuit or by sensors/devices on the weapons themselves to enable things like:
+ Vision modes (zoom, x-ray, thermal, infrared, dark vision, bio detector, etc. smartly turning on and being used as needed per situation.)
+ Neural control or intuitive yet safe gesture control for targeting, aiming, firing and mode selection, etc.
+ Ammo/weapon status info link to visor hud
+ Security lock to prevent enemy capture and use of weapons or to activate safety, especially on annihilation class weapons.
+ Rails, connectors, sockets, plugs, to either mount, dock or place a weapon upon a rack or workbench for storage or maintenance.
+ Weapons may also have convenient covers to either protect against elements or to open up the chassis for repairs.
+ Signal lights and sound elements if weapon is lost nearby and operator is unsure where the gun is hidden. Like call your phone to find out where it is.

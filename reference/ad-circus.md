# Ad Circus

A recurring monetisation concept in Reactorcore's designs: an ethical, non-intrusive way to show adverts. Several designs mention "Ad Circus" in a menu list or a business section without explaining it. This file is the shared reference, so a page or article writer knows what the term means.

## The idea

The game never interrupts the player with an ad. Instead it has a dedicated **Ad Circus** screen, separate from the rest of the game, that people open voluntarily whenever they feel like it. It shows a small grid of 4–8 adverts (piped from Google Ads or a similar network) that cycles to fresh entries every few hours. Nobody sees an ad unless they walk into the circus.

In Reactorcore's words (2026-10-23): "hey, ads let me discover new things, but I only wanna view it when I wanna view it, so I'm gonna go check out the Ad Circus to see what's on offer there today and maybe I'll find something cool/interesting or just laugh at crappy ads, hence the 'ad circus' name."

## The detailed version (Shape Smasher, scan SS14)

The fullest sketch is a mobile screen in Shape Smasher's notes (`C:\08 Projects\Project_ShapeSmasher\Scan_Shapesmasher\SS14.jpg`, digested in `designs/done/shapesmasher/structure.md`), equally usable on PC or web:

- **The screen**: a grid of ad tiles drawn as closed theatre curtains, with a banner "New ads every 6 hours!", an "About the ad circus" section, a **support progress bar** (e.g. "support data 42/100") and a support disclaimer/notice button.
- **Viewing**: open a tile to see its content. Close at any time; no obligation to watch fully. Viewing an ad for at least 5 seconds counts as a valid view and registers toward the support progress. The viewer has a link, a "more info" button where applicable, a 5-second timer and a close button.
- **The info pop-up** explains what the support goes toward: new features in development, current server costs and data limits, expenses and funding goals, impact and communication.
- **Entry types**: (a) standardised ads defined by the ad network; (b) custom-made ads: image, text (title, description), link(s), video. No interactive content inside the circus; a link can lead to some, but outside the circus.
- **Rules**: keep it out of the way and optional; communicate clearly what it is, why it's there and what it gives both sides; keep it fun and casual (maybe a passing, gentle poke at other games' ad practices); link to future developments and say that any support goes toward those aims. Only urge people to watch if they want to for themselves.

## Placement

SS14 says "not main menu, only as a submenu in Options or Extras". TopVerse's main menu (scan B2) lists Ad Circus as a main menu item, and Reactorcore's 2026-10-23 explanation describes a dedicated page on the main menu. The constant across all versions: it is its own screen, reached only on purpose, and never shown during play.

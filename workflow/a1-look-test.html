Where fun actually comes from

Reactorcore6 min read

This block shows how long-form text reads in the new theme: articles, the book and the AAL wiki will all use it. The words here are placeholders for the look, not final copy.

A game should fulfil fundamental human needs, because that's where what people call "fun" actually comes from. Competence, autonomy, belonging, a sense of progress: when a game meets these, players keep coming back without being tricked into it.

The industry keeps recycling stale ideas. More people forking these designs into their own versions means more variety and better games.

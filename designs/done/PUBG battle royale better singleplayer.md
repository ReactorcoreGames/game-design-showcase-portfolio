General Gameplay loop:
- Drop in (or teleport in terminator style) from air or side, (assuming planetary surface map, alternatively could have a zero g spacemap), either naked or with loadout.
- Blow up target structures and mobs around the map, avoid/utilize in map hazards/opportunities.
- Avoid miasma or neutron bombardment (enclosing ring, but also momentary area denial blasts or lingering hotspots that temporarily render big areas untraversible without some form of sacrifice).
- loot guns, ammo, devices, gear, armor, vehicles, specials to power up or replace spent gear.
- goal is to take out the area's neutron/miasma capability to liberate the zone from terror. Usually they're structures or mobile units dotted randomly around the map.
- relatively fast gameplay but more player initiated; instead of storm ring that shrinks towards a random location, miasma nodes around the map define the ring/oval boundaries. If you take out nodes on one side, the ring shrinks down to combined radius of remaining nodes.

V2 - refocus to just cause 2 / fallout 3 style location cleanup. Go destroy a mafia member by member like the punisher; cause chaos, flush em out, kill em. Rescue oppressed civvies from military forces. Go paragon or renegade. (Keep civvies safe... or not).
- randomly generate map (planetary square region 6x6 or 12x12 grid, about 1 square = 1km) (space cube region 4x4x4 or 8x8x8) both having starfox 2 or freelancer style map layout affected by bordering regions. Heuristic tree algorithm of if else statements with minor chaos randomization chance. Parameters for height, temperature, humidity, pollution/radiation/desolation, for planetary maps, determines forests, plains, lakes, mountains, desert, etc areas naturally. Parameters for space include density, presence of elements (rock, water, inert gas (hydrogen), reactive gas (methane), scrap debris small or large, common minerals (iron, gold, etc), exotic minerals (uranium, elerium, eezo, etc), heat (via sun or other major ambient source), energy/radiation (either as safe ionization to alter enviro or as a damaging hazard ala Tau 31 radiation from freelancer).
- on map, place major locations with objectives to knock out cold and minor locations for extra random rewards and additional stuff to blow up. Just Cause 2 props; statues, comm towers, fuel silos/tanks/barrels, ammo depots, propaganda billboards/trucks, pumps/generators/reactors/transformers/hubs/servers, parked vehicles, barracks, food supply, water supply, logistics warehouses/stockpiles, Factorio stuff like forges, assemblers, refineries, robot control hubs, autominers, satellite dishes/antennae, SAM sites, artillery systems, weapons platforms, missile silos, turrets, jammers, shield generators, factory components like HVAC, smoke stacks, waste disposal sheds, recycling bins, lab equipment, computers, vats, sensitive electronic components, large batteries, alarm systems, hazard system controllers (buzzsaws, crushers, fire jets, etc), turret controllers, and such. Cryptark stuff.
- placement happens via similar heuristic tree algorithm of if else statements. Determine location type, dispense budget of things, spend it randomly on suitable items and place them in a first come first serve style rules. Oh yeah, place enemy unit spawns here too afterwards where empty spaces were reserved.
- goal is to have a large square area region of map that player can roam with undiscovered locations icons dotting the land ala fallout 3 with explorer perk, giving a clear view of places to visit and once there, switch to just cause 2 style 100% location completed style where you blow stuff up, find the things with a hotter-colder detector with icons that mark things you gotta wipe out. Mafia members are randomly scattered in locations and player doesn't know where they are at but they can find clues (holotapes) that tell where they are or just stumble into them at the location.
- Taking out leaders/bosses/lords has a supercore like effect; take one out and their bonuses that were bolstering the grip on the area and powering their goons is now turned into a chaos trait that weakens or makes remaining goons more unpredictable/desperate (stupid, reckless, fearful, panic, AWOL, confused, blind, slower or apathetic/depressed, do suicide).
- Holotapes in general communicate various things: coordinates for special loot, weak points of location ("bruh, we stored way too many fuel barrels here"), classified information ("there an experimental ion tank in this base yo"), feelings of crew (fear level against player), thoughts of crew (despicable stuff usually, sometimes mixed with humanities, to show the mindset of the people and also challenge player to see that they will still have human qualities but not necessarily redeemable in some cases), interesting quotes ("when you're pushed, killing is as easy as breathing"), propaganda/lies (help player learn what propaganda actually looks like).

Thoughts:
- generally I want to capture the essence of the physics characters from TABG and generally keep the player pacing somewhere between fallout 3, just cause 2, and soldat, with an emphasis on the latter, introducing jet boots as default equipment that can be used for higher jumps, supermaning, backflips, kneeslide ala Vanquish, maybe skiing like in Tribes, but overall making the speed of action generally feel slower, closer to just cause 2, with the extra addition of slomo and even Vats time freeze to slow things down even more on demand. I'd also want there to be momentum ala classic Doom II.
- Camera wise I'd have fallout 3 camera switch between fps and tps views, but using just cause 2 tps camera.
- as a player I want them to be sort of a naked character that can grab/interact anything, carry/use items, wear gear, drive anything, jump/do parkour/crouch/crawl. I could have a SPECIAL system to have different character archetypes that have bonuses or weaknesses in certain areas, like strength, endurance, perception, agility, affecting proficiency with different types of items, weapons, character speed and... Maybe just use Streets of Rogue character builder; 4 main stats for HP, speed, melee, ranged + perks + status effects.
- as an open world roamer, player would need an inventory atleast on behalf of the hotbar slots and holotapes. I like SoR's inventory and weapon selector with it's slowmo so I'd use that, but with an added weight system where each item has mass and progressively slows down character. Character has limited carry slots (though some items stack with a max stack size before needing to occupy another slot) but some items are heavy that carrying even a few copies of them will cause mobility penalties upon the character like:
- reduces jump/flight time
- slower grapple pull
- slower turn
- slower walk
- slow run
- shorter slide
- slower ledge pull
- slower slide on level surfaces or uphill (especially!) but faster on slopes
- backflips practically useless
- roll slow
- slower crouch walk/prone crawl
- faster fall speed
- reduced recoil/knockback from hits/harder to be pushed by others
- bigger body slam impact power
Press E to pickup or swap currently held item, can drop/throw currently held item or item from inventory based if shift is held down.

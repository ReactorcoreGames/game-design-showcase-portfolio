+ waypoint system

Goals:
Eliminate the stress of real time control.
Reduce or remove micromanagement as much as possible. (Both rts and aw kind)
Make cultivation of base building, logistics and battle plans encouraged, valued, cherished and reusable.
Add physics to play via explosions, knockback, recoil, pulling, pushing/shoving, torque, gravity.
Make terrain malleable and editable.
Still have the same 4 layer system (ug, surface, aerial, orbit) or 2 layer space (inner, outer).
Also use same view as AWB and graphics, using aw2 unit/world objects scaling of all things.
Prevent a "Simon says" experience in terms of gameplay flow, faction design, unit design, tech/research design, that lacks freedom and creativity. Either create a highly generic but endlessly expandable AW2 type unit roster with CO passives determining faction or make an armored core style unit and CO builder that you commit to for the next battle(s), or offer both options. Maybe these complement each other where the latter works like mod user content workshop directly integrated into the game like the worms world party weapon builder.
Combat is determined via guaranteed aw style attacks with no RNG involved.
Feel should be a mix of comfy and exciting.
Integration of a persistent universe metagame with persistent characters (nemesis like system, both ally, neutral and enemy), events (local, regional, global), revisitable places (home, sites, enemy ruins) and the overall rest of the metagame system of AWB.
Simplify entire game to being a choice of what to use, in what amount/combo/style, where and when. Resource management is auto, building is semi-auto (you paint the layout of the base and order of construction with manual overrides when needed, only adjusting stuff.) unit production is based on mission type -
A type: choose your roster you want to maintain (constant auto production and replenishment)
B type: limited resources mode where you maintain an XCOM style squad but with factories, like AW2.
Be inspired by line wars for simplicity of logistics, but use AW2 style combat powered by rts automations and multiple selection, mass move attack, with waypoints.
Game is easy - more akin to bob the builder solving neighborhood problems than an intimidating foe that is scary to take on. No Sturms, only a bunch of mooks equal or weaker than you. They never cheat either.

This particular spinoff project is mainly inspired by two crude violent Adobe Flash games that used to be on Newgrounds in the early 2000's; 'Skull Kid' and 'Whack Your Boss'. Both games were designed for anger management without having to act out on those feelings in real life. They and many games like that personally helped me mentally withstand psychological abuse and emotional violence during my early teenage years when pressures, expectations and frustrating hypocrisy from parents, school staff, strangers and society were unbearable at times. They were also useful in the sense of showing violence in an uncensored way which let me see how horrible real violence actually is, making it less appealing to see or do irl, compared to something sanitized and cartoonified like Epic Game's Fortnite which paradoxically normalizes and encourages violence by sugarcoating it.

# Info on Game 1:
'Skull Kid' is a funny and slightly macabre game for everybody, who wants to let off some steam. Walk around the office as the merciless psycho Skull Kid (a young dude with a literal skull for a head) and destroy everything and everyone in your way. Use deadly weapons such as chainsaws and firearms. Your goal is to walk all the way to the exit door and get to the next level without getting killed by the office workers. First level has no threats to you, middle levels have a mix of passive targets and active targets that attack back with knives and modern firearms, while last level is a passive CEO boss that chastises Skull Kid but is too lazy and arrogant to fight or even think that theyre in any kind of danger.

Take your chainsaw and firearms and destroy everything that crosses your way, such as tables, couches, copy machines or even humans. Only if you make it to the exit door, your task is accomplished and you can move on to the next level. But be careful, soon your targets will defend themselves and you might be in serious danger yourself.

# Info on Game 2:
'Whack Your Boss' is a simple beat the boss game with a dark and sense of humor. the game is focused on you beating, fighting and killing your boss. How will your frustrated employee respond to the supervisor's condescending attitude and frustrating ticks? A very simple idea: What would happen if you were to unleash your pent-up anger on your boss? The answer comes in the form a crude, yet surprisingly violent gameplay of one guy straight up killing his boss and any other goons that get in their way. Each boss battle is a crazy, and bloody depiction of revenge and over-the-top carnage.

Whack Your Boss is a morbidly fun boss fighting game. Make an emotionally unstable, psychopathic employee turn everyday office items into deadly murder weapons. Watch him beat, bludgeon and skewer his boss with all kinds of objects, lying around at the workplace. Explore your character's workplace to find new ways to kill, maim or whack your boss during business hours. Laugh at the sadistic and gruesome ways the physical attacks on your boss turn lethal. It's all the makings of a serial killer, from beating, shooting and just wacking an unsuspecting boss.

Some notable differences compared to other spinoff games:
- Title is 'Skullhead: Psycho Murder Spree'
- Dismemberment is accompanied with blood spray particle effects, and dismembered limbs can be further damaged to change their surface texture, ranging from intact skin to bruised, wounded, mutilated and gored with bone visible, after which those limbs become immune to attack/projectile collisions, likely to settle somewhere on the floor.
- Player has a unique ability to regain health and regrow lost limbs by dismembering or killing live characters. Or by consuming food items found in the level. Only player can heal in this game while opponents cannot, to balance out the fact that player goes up against many opponents and obstacles all alone.
- Instead of being arenas, stages are structured as horizontal stages akin to NES Super Mario, albeit without bottomless pits or tilebased terrain. They're instead littered with static or physics props, some destroyable, some as hard terrain/walls.
- Generally the theme and style of the game is satirical and ironic akin to the game 'Postal 2', taking place in the early 1990's cubicle offices, mid-tier malls, boring cow farms, various typical city and suburban locations, trailer parks, prisons, fast food places, etc.
- The color scheme a mix of boring urban muted colors with a splash of red stained blood. No neon colors or glows.
- Player chooses a starting loadout of 2 weapons and can swap them (by pressing E) with whatever they find on the stage. Weapon have infinite ammo and durability mostly for the sake of keeping the game simple instead of worrying about ammo management or durability.
- Player can also press E to activate context sensitive objects like doors or use items on the ground such as food items.

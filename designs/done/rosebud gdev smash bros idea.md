Closed room smash bros.

Beat something up until it reaches high enough percentage damage and deal a finishing blow to break it through the surfaces.

If shot sideways, do a funny animation of a mountain getting obliterated by the flying victim.
If shot down, either send to hell or break out the other side of planet or just make a small hole that causes a tombstone to pop out briefly and plug the hole neatly, marking a grave.
If shot up, do anim of victim flying into sun and going poof.
Or hit and cause them to vaporize, explode, gib, etc.

Emphasis on combos as each hit gives a delay that is reset with each new hit, allowing the player to stack damage and multiply it before either a delay is shortened too far (each delay is shorter than last one) or if DPS or accumulated damage exceeds limit.

Victim is some target of annoyance that the player gets to wail at and bounce around the level and it's walls+hazards until its broken or smashed out of room with extreme force.

It's a stress relief game with smash bros controls and perhaps Kirby like ability switching sets via item grabs or MegaMan style weapon selection system.

Player controls
+ Wasd, move/aim/crouch 
+ h, atk
+ j, special
+ k, defend/block/dodge
+ L, jump
+ esc, pause menu
+ F, grab/use/equip/throw/drop
+ q, mode/weapon/loadout change

Most buttons can be pressed on their own for basic atk/def/ability/grab/func, while pressing a direction with it will cause a power move in that direction.

Most atk is spawn a collider, apply directional force to obj in it, apply directional force to self, apply damage %, run cooldown before another attack or action can be made.
Projectiles are the same except the collider moves attached to it's own. Player can use existing momentum to "slide" their attack to cover a wider area.

Hazards include damagers like saws, beams, bumpers and other active force projecting things. I could do spikes and lava, but they're a bit static to bounce things off. They're better as finisher things, things outside the main room walls to impale smash or slam victims into.

Some ideas for the victim thing:
- a random car SF2 bonus style, but it flies all over the place as it gradually becomes more badly mangled until it explodes.
- polygon team but they're replaced with something else that get knocked out easily.
- a char that stay on middle of level as static heavy object.
- a lightweight thing that bounces easily.
- a variant that is pulled to the center, resist being moved away.
- crocomire type char that keeps being pushed back towards ledge until it falls off.
- a stack of barrels/crates whatever.

Note: none of the "opponents" pose any pressure or danger upon the player character. Player can freely whack them with no retaliation in full safety. The act of bashing the victim should feel spectacular and bombastic, making it fun on its own regardless of victory. Game mainly provides some dynamism to make the scenario not boring, static or irritating to grind on. Again, it's a stress relief toy, not a smash bros like challenge game. Maybe more akin to nanaca crash where you try to smash something up to a ridiculous degree and see how far it flies. Maybe literally make it into an incremental game ala hill climb racing or something.

I'm thinking that the player character is akin to Goku, some funny bootleg Saiyan god that is utterly overpowered and over the top, similar to the style in the Sonic's Quest for Ultimate Power series, loud, Brazilian-energy, explosive, bombastic to an excessive degree. Maybe even hilariously edgy and intimidating in an tongue in cheek way. Newgrounds style.

Meta game wise there's not much beyond a standard gauntlet of 10 levels, or a single level with incremental mechanics. It could be later adopted to a challenge game about moving around and brawling, dueling, free-for-all or boss fights (single, warning forever style, boss rush). But then again, what would be the purpose... Perhaps a defeat the henchmen, then the king type of thing? A revenge/justice plot could be fun. It should be a relatable and defeatable definitive target with a clear ending. Maybe a vigilante that constantly puts down dark zones of shadow like a nightrider.

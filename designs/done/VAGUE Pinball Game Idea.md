Original thought:

Basically I saw pinball gameplay on reddit and thought hey what if claude helped me make a pinball game but without the death pit? Instead, keep hitting pinball around level until it shatters. Keep simple controls and keep the single launch/flipper area, letting the ball do most of the work with the whole typical satisfying concept of watching the ball bounce, fly, smack stuff and eventually return back to the launcher where you try to keep the combo going by timing your shots right and maybe influencing the ball midflight with minimal air control, or using slow motion with course corrective whacks ala Nanaca Crash (either to boost forward momentum or apply a force push in chosen direction).

Premise could be future scifi war situation where a kamikaze war drone broke into an enemy starship, plugged into their reactor control interface and is using this "pinball" weapon thing to destabilize the core by smashing specific components or scoring enough hits/damage upon the reactorcore to cause a total meltdown and explode the entire enemy starship.

Player would kick the pinball around the level, hitting combos to increase speed and force, making the ball cause more damage to targets the faster it is along with gaining more self-damage resistance. Good play is when player succeeds at getting lucky at chaining multiple hits, reaching bonus objectives that are on the level that provide boosts, open gates or remove obstacles. Bad play is when player misses targets and fails to reach, leading the ball's "magnetic field" to dissipate and fail, causing the ball to break earlier than desired.

Player has infinite balls, but good play yields more score which results in bigger post-game scene that shows the enemy ship blowing up as hard as the score the player managed to accumulate for that level.

Maps work mostly like typical pinball boards, except there is no launcher that drops the ball from top and there is no death pit at the bottom or those side canals that would normally lead to a death pit. Theres gravity pulling downwards and at the bottom there is a player "ball launcher and rebounder" device of some kind that launches the ball initially and later keeps kicking it whenever it falls or bounces back down.

Alternative ideas: No gravity at all. Ball keeps losing momentum from walls and breaks if too slow. Speeds up when hits valid targets.
Mixed with breakout: bricks that break when hit either X amount of times or with X amount of force or a combination of two. Level is still a mix of pinball board with bumpers, launcher slots the ball can reach and be kicked out of at great speed (and chosen direction). Bricks are semi-randomly placed.

Levels are a mix of custom made templates (one for each starship core drive type) and some randomized elements, like extra bumpers, breakable bricks, special core related events, weather or other special phenomena caused by the target core of the pinball board.

Soldat Doom Infinite

Player respawns endlessly on death
Weapons have infinite ammo, Start with All Weapons, Use original Stats.
Special Weapons have limited ammo
Ammo Pickups give grenades or slow motion juice, reset weapon cooldowns
Jet Boots (Recharging)
Advanced Movement Abilities (jump based momentum movement boosting, backflip, prone)
Focus on judicious use of slow motion (as an on-demand ability ala Max Payne)
UT2k4 Adrenalin style powers

Lore:
A bunch of guys decided to kill hell and you can be one of them. 
(thats it.)

Basic melee attack (usable with any weapon, extra damage with knife and saw.)

General weapon power is rated for HellKnight/Arachno/Fatso = Soldat time to kill. This means most enemies go down very fast and in few shots. Imps,zombies,pinkies are basically chow. Player themselves only has default 100hp, plus body armor pickups that provide 90% protection for 200(green) and 500(blue) points, but both are rare. Stimpacks heal 50%, Medkits 100%.

health vials give slow mo
kills and armor shards give ut2k4 style adrenaline to trigger those abilities (regen, speed(move and jump), flight (endless jet boots), rate of fire 2x)

player start with random primary and secondary + grenades.

All weapon pickups are randomized, except BFG that gives Special Weapons (with full ammo refill)

powerups give predator (blur), flamegod (soul), rambo (invul), stat + heavy armor (mega), night vision (goggle), hazard protect (radsuit), berserk (6x damage, 30 secs) (ditto). All powerups are inventory pickups that player can choose when to use. Stats are adjusted for Doom gameplay (longer durations).

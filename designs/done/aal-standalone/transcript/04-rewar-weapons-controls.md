## Glossary additions

- **Combat jet mode / jet form**: the ReWaR's flight configuration: the rifle mounted down the centre of the round shield with the robot behind it (R18, R30). Four ways of shifting into it are sketched (A–D on R30).
- **Gerwalk-type legs**: legs that swing forward in jet form, as in Macross (R20, *interpretation*).
- **Par gen / particle generator**: the rifle's core, fed by the ReWaR's reactor through a power port. **P-Cap**: particle capacitor. **E-Cap**: excess energy store. **Activator**: the stage that weaponises and charges the particles before launch; it opens up in Buster mode (R25).
- **Mutator / particle intake**: the rifle parts that take in and alter particles (R21).
- **EMF control / EMF-C / magnetic field generators**: the barrel rails that accelerate the shot and set its speed, shape and spread (R22, R25, R28).
- **AA laser / atmospheric annihilation laser**: a laser that clears the air ahead of the shot to extend the particle projectiles' range (R21, R22).
- **Buster mode**: the rifle docked to the back reactor port, unlimited activator capacity: wide raw particle beam, concentrated cutting beam ("massive laser sword"), buster bolt ("aka nuke"). Vulnerable, and switching modes takes time (R24, R25).
- **Bleeding trail / EM bleeding**: the beam sheds lesser charged particles and arcs, damaging near misses (R24, R26).
- **Pilebunker**: the left forearm's spike, combined with a folding heat/vibroblade (R30). **Bomb gun**: launches fusion bombs from the shield (R31).
- **Barrier generator**: the shield's particle-based energy barrier, "first line of defense" (R31); the torso also has an energy barrier generator for heat and cold (R19).
- **Side attachers / impact cap**: the fusion bomb's sticking parts: claw/spikes, magnet, expanding adhesive foam (R32, R33).
- **Control states**: the mothership's Automatic, Semiauto (UT2004 Raptor-style) and Manual (Freelancer-style) piloting modes (R34).
- **Catapult / linear catapult**: the mothership's launcher for the ReWaR; the shield's landing gear is compatible with it (R31, R34).

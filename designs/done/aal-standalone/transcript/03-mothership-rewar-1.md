## Glossary additions

- **CATA**: label on R2, probably the catapult (the mothership's "Linear Catapult" for launching the ReWaR, R13).
- **LAB**: mothership compartment that constructs drones (R5); also shown with a "lab basement (bottom exit)" (R2).
- **RVs**: resource values, consumed by drone building, ship repairs (R5) and railgun ammo (R8).
- **Repair positions**: six numbered slots on the mothership hull where repair drones sit (R5).
- **Drone store / Exit airlock / Storage**: mothership rooms in the ecosystem diagram (R5).
- **FM**: prefix on weapon modes (R8), probably "fire mode".
- **RoF**: rate of fire.
- **AoE / AA style**: area-of-effect, anti-aircraft style flak.
- **PD turrets**: point-defence turrets.
- **GRS1**: "GRS1 style laser system" (R8), an outside reference, meaning not given.
- **L2–L5**: numbers after each flight thruster (R9), probably a size/power level.
- **Frontal Energy Projector**: shield during re-entry, acceleration field during liftoff (R9).
- **ECM suite**: reveals cloaked enemies (R4). **Exterior Eyes**: target things near the ship. **Defense Barrier Projectors**.
- **Megabomb (guided)**, **CM gun**, **Manip. arm.**: parts of a weapon pod (R11).
- **Buster Rifle / Mega Sword / Bayonet**: extra ReWaR weapons added to the Arm Rifle line (R12).
- **RCS**: reaction control system (shoulder thrusters, R15).
- **Tail Binder**: the back unit's balance-control role (R15), likely a Gundam reference.
- **VTOL**: vertical take-off and landing (breast thrusters, R15).
- **ATR**: Automatic Target Recognision (R16). **IFF**: identification friend or foe (R16).
- **Head styles** (R16): flag, gundam, hardballer, vofom (Votoms?), AC (Armored Core?), crusader, iron giant, big red eye, camera array.
- **CM guns**: countermeasure guns built into the knee plates (3x) and side pieces (2x) (R17).
- **Knee cap thruster**, **side thruster (RCS)**, **front piece**, **sidepiece** (R17).

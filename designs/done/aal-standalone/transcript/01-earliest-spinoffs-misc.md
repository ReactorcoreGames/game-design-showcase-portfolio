# Standalone notebook transcript: Earliest ideas, spinoffs, loose sheets and the oldest pages

This chunk covers, in order: the five "earliest raw ideas" scans (E1–E5: "SA concept #1" structure mind map, a GunMetal-style environment sketch, a mod-friendliness note, a sheet of early wheeled-robot and weapon sketches, and the "World 1" / "Art direction" pitch sheet); six spinoff and offshoot pages (X1–X6: "Standalone 2D editions", the three "AoY – Cyberspace Alternative" roguelite pages, "User Creative Tools", "Standalone 2"); four loose misc sheets (M1–M4: a render-layer sketch, two printed AoY asset lists with handwritten additions, a pink sticky note of mesh tools); and three old "Features of Standalone" pages (R35–R37) describing a piloted single mech with a super reactor, merged super-rifle and close-range energy blade modes. Several of these pages describe an earlier version of the concept in which "you pilot a Standalone" (a single piloted mech or wheeled robot, sometimes with a supply copter/dropship) rather than the later AI-controlled mothership + ReWaR tandem.

## Glossary additions

- **SA concept #1**: the earliest structure of the game: a Scenario System (worlds, scripts, missions, epic battles/challenges, story campaign) plus Assets (Standalone + pilot, military/civilian others, world maps/deco), all moddable.
- **GunMetal**: the 2002 mech/jet game, cited as the model for a bounded open environment with inaccessible background terrain.
- **Supply copter/dropship**: in the earliest pitch (E5), the support craft for the player's robot; a forerunner of the mothership.
- **Leg support (parking or artillery)**: a fold-out strut on the early wheeled robot that braces it for parking or artillery fire.
- **Standalone 2D editions**: a 2D side-view spinoff with a vertically looping layered game space, 1 km horizontal chunks with population %, circular space maps (distance sectors + angle chunks + core), and "Horizon Core" far-vision backgrounds.
- **Cyberspace Alternative**: an AoY roguelite spinoff (Gungeon/Isaac style): linear finite runs with a barebones ReWaR, randomizable content and advanced randomization presets; "cyberspace" justifies the artificial structure.
- **Wormhole chase assault / Drop Tunnel Assault / Rä[?]force Campaign**: campaign shapes sketched for the Cyberspace Alternative (or a linear campaign).
- **Reverse upgrade mode**: start with a random overpowered character and discard upgrades to continue.
- **Camera Actor**: an indestructible cheat-mode camera unit for user creative tools (time control, spawn/kill, NV/thermal, 360°).
- **SS mode**: screenshot mode (HUD off, visual settings).
- **Standalone 2**: an offshoot mech concept with arm modules (manipulator, emitter, main gun, passive shields) and leg modules (propulsion, auto 360 turret, dashing system, passive shields).
- **FG/BG Filter**: render layers in front of and behind the game layer (M1).
- **Mesh Colorer / Mesh Exploder / Mesh Baker**: planned tools: character recolouring, an "overkill" mesh-shatter effect, mesh baking.
- **Central super reactor**: in the old piloted-SA concept, the reactor powering everything (life support, propulsion, shields, weapons).
- **e-based ball shield**: energy-based spherical passive shield.
- **OC system**: overcharge system on the back of the SA that lets the merged super rifle charge "virtually limitless" power, venting waste heat (collateral damage), forming an aura around the SA.
- **Super rifle / main merged gun**: both arm guns merged; long charge, massive blast beam, shockwave, recovery delay, abortable.
- **Close-range weapon modes**: 1. solid long blade, 2. wide energy emitter ray, 3. whip blade array, 4. shield emitter.

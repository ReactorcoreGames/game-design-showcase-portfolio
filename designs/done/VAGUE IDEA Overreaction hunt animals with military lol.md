# Game title: "Human Apex: Overreacting Justice Against Animal Kingdom"

## Project Idea
Destroy Animals with literal military hardware because the animal did something bad to human in community. Aka stuff like 'wild wolf ate a child, bear mauled a woman, shark ate an athlete, snake poisoned the village elder, crazy deer dropkicked a kid, a goat headbutted a person of a cliff, fox ate all the chickens in a farm, lion/tiger devoured a whole family, mosquito gave man ebola/malaria, a bat bit a researcher in a lab and gave them the coronavirus, an owl hunted down a dog owner's poor dog', etc.

The tone is lightly humorous and absurd and the gameplay is basically a cathartic curb stomp of experience of showing the animal kingdom who's the true apex lifeform on this planet - especially given the history of ancestors that were constantly under threat from wild life doing awful things to people, either due to territory, predation or hostility.


## Gameplay
Its not a single soldier survival type of thing. There is access to heavily armored and armed tactical soldiers that can eat a wolf for breakfast, even including driving military vehicles and using various military weapons/hardware, switching between units, controlling individuals, commanding groups or vehicles or other things.

Its not a challenge game. Essentially its a curbstomp fest where first command identifies an area with wolves, fences it off so they cant escape and then let you go and turn them into fine paste.

The action of slaying the wolves is supposed to be satisfying. They gib, yelp and whimper in fear, sometimes running away, sometimes trying to fight, only really able to disable/stun any soldier without ever having a real chance of killing them.

Not a puzzle game. Player's goal is to simply find and mop up a bunch of wolves and blow up the area in the process optionally. Player gets a bunch of tools and uses them based on preference. Its like an interactive toy where you go to mess around, feel overpowered and burn away anger.

Game itself could be done in 2D top down shooter with light RTS elements or 3D FPS akin to Battlefield 2, albeit as a singleplayer experience where each player classes allow for commanding squadmates in a similar way like in Mass Effect 2 and each player class or controllable character is fairly powerful as a standalone unit with any sort of team work being fairly optional. Latter would be better and allow for improved size differences of player vs animals.

## New terms

- **LVIS**: appears as "LVIS packs" (embedded functionality packs injected into a house) and "buildings (LVIS)". Meaning not given; *(interpretation)* a building/structure functionality module system (possibly "Living/Vehicle/Interior System" or similar); look for an expansion elsewhere.
- **RSH**: in "Low Poly [RSH]"; unexplained. *(interpretation)* possibly "Reactorcore Style H..." or a label for the low-poly art system covering all object classes.
- **Res Tri / Res Ball / Res Mats**: resource-material shapes: a solid material chunk becomes a resource triangle with a full tri collider (obstructs BG elements), loose material a resource ball with a dot collider and obstruction radius.
- **RU triangle / RU ball**: the form resources take after digging (Type-A destabilize instantly at 0% structural HP; Type-B gradual harvester extraction, stored internally).
- **BG override tris (interiors) / BG mode**: background-layer triangles that replace a terrain triangle's back with an interior; objects can drop into a background mode.
- **Interior tri**: one interior triangle per terrain triangle; merged multi-tri shapes (square = 2, pentagon = 3, hexagon = 4).
- **Multitris / tri merging**: terrain triangles merging naturally, by chaos, or intentionally into larger polygons.
- **Exclusion / Negative / Reverse meshes (Excluders)**: subtractive meshes cutting holes/tunnels/damage into solid mass; if over 80% (var X) of a solid is excluded, it and its excluders are deleted.
- **Inspection Mode (LOD -1)**: a closer-than-full-detail view of one object at a time showing markings, writing, greebles, dirt, wear, grunge, as in-game clues/history.
- **Principle of significance**: LOD 0–4 ladder (all + interior → no decor → silhouette → box → data point) chosen by size, activity, significance and main/sub object status.
- **Embedded functionality packs / capability injection packs**: modules injected into a structure to give it functions (e.g. house + LVIS = 50% functionality; facade pack = 1 building module).
- **Privacy Facade**: a toggleable cover on hollow terrain/vehicles/stations/houses: outsiders cannot see in, insiders see all; astral powers can nullify it.
- **Object internal functionality canvas**: a node-editor lab where inventions (devices), filters and utilities, derived from natural phenomena/forces, are wired into an object.
- **X-EFFECT**: one of the natural phenomena icons; unexplained.
- **E-G-L tris**: energy/gas/liquid triangles.
- **BG-MG shifting**: moving characters/items/vehicles/buildings between background and midground layers.
- **Modder Content Complexity Check**: Chaos System check at game start for size, activity potential, significance and part count of modded content.
- **Time Skip**: forward-only time travel (stasis, cryostorage, time machines, chaos anomaly portals) using the passage-of-time system.
- **Memory Aquizition**: listed beside Memory and Astral Beings; meaning not given.

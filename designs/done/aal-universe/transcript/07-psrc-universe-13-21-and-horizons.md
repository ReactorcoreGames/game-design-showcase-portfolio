## PS-16 — [Pressure & shattering, quakes, overlap mechanics, solid chunk dynamics]
Source: `Image (16).jpg`

The top two notes are upside down; read from a rotated copy.

### Note 1 (top left): Pressure & shattering (S)

**Text:**

- Solids can store forces imparted upon them, which if they can, will wobble/shake/jump (not pressed from all sides) (Raycast ↔↕) to relieve it if the forces reach a threshold.
- If forces are too intense, then (and material is weak/brittle/loose) the body will shatter into smaller orphan components that either get absorbed by others or become settled independently if density permits it. (local)

### Note 2 (top right): Quakes (S)

**Text:**

Two types

- a) Wave motion ⇉ - push of force
- b) Vibration ↙⇄↗ - rumble of pressure release
- If too strong quake, chunk, region, continent, celestial etc. will shatter - and perhaps explode, depending on material.
- Can happen on micro/macro level. (chunk - celestial)
- Macro can command micro, while events initiated at micro level may impact macro.

**Diagrams:** Small arrow glyphs: three parallel arrows for wave motion, a jittery multi-direction arrow cluster for vibration.

### Note 3 (bottom left): Overlap mechanics (S/L/G) (S marked A, G marked B)

**Text:**

- When chunks/regions/continents overlap, they generate and store pressure on how deep they've overlapped into another body + how large the bodies are.
- Normally natural forces resolve these overlaps peacefully and slowly by moving overlapped bodies apart.
- However severe overlaps may generate a quake; a strong violent kick to resolve the overlap.
- A quake may be strong enough to shatter a body, causing its refuse to be absorbed by other nearby bodies.

### Note 4 (bottom right): Solid Chunk Dynamics

**Text (labels):** Quakes vs. Pressure · shake · shatter · No overlap = strenghtened SOLIDS MOVE AS ONE EVEN AT CHUNK LEVEL.

- Applied to chunk directly or via region (and above) Forces
- Overlap = move as individuals.

**Diagrams:** Left: a circle with wavy vibration marks around it (quake). Right: a square with arrows pointing outward in all directions from its centre and wavy marks (pressure). Below: a cluster of overlapping circles labelled "shake"; a jumble of tilted overlapping squares; and a neat 4×2 grid of squares labelled "shatter" with the boxed note that non-overlapping (grid-aligned) solids are strengthened and move as one even at chunk level.

**Notes:** Overlap → pressure → quake → shatter is the core solid-terrain physics loop; shattered "orphan" pieces are absorbed or settle, consistent with equivalent exchange.

## New terms

- **S/L/G**: Solid / Liquid / Gas, the three matter states; notes are tagged (S), (L), (L/G), (S/L/G) by which state they apply to.
- **Atmo/geo cell, planet partition**: a planet is split into 8 cells (an octagon of rects) running from the ground up through the atmosphere; cells can be culled/added at the "corners" so planets shrink or grow.
- **Atmo structure L1/L2/L3**: atmosphere layers: L1 one circle, L2 eight rects, L3 each rect subdivided into 1–128 cells by celestial size; cells equalize density with neighbours, making wind.
- **Atmospheric blend zone / ceiling**: where atmosphere fades into space; the ceiling is the outer limit of a planet's partition.
- **Grav/mag sphere (magnetic+gravity sphere/boundary)**: a celestial's outer field boundary; for a star it is "star system wide".
- **Frosting chunks**: ice, foam etc. floating on an ocean/liquidsphere.
- **Liquidsphere / hydrosphere**: the liquid layer of a celestial.
- **Overlap mechanics**: overlapping bodies store pressure by overlap depth and size, normally push apart slowly, severe overlaps cause quakes.
- **Quake (wave motion / vibration)**: a force event that resolves overlap; can shatter bodies; works at micro (chunk) and macro (celestial) level, "macro can command micro".
- **Orphan components**: shattered fragments that get absorbed by other bodies or settle independently.
- **Supercelestial**: the top size solids can reach; liquids and gases can go further, up to galactic clouds.
- **Liquid-Gas Canvas System**: the separate system of overlapping circular liquid/gas chunks and regions; liquids act as trigger areas, never rigid colliders.
- **E-Fields / "Force" fields**: overlay fields (radiation, electricity, bacteria, magnetism, pressure, heat...), either celestial-wide or as local projections shaped like a circle, ray, beam, rect, line or point.
- **Exclusion aura**: a bubble of normal conditions a digging machine/device carries through crust/mantle/core; stops when digging stops, leaving things stuck.
- **Exclusion zone (rings)**: the gap a growing celestial's gravity core clears in a collection, leaving a ring.
- **Solid fields / collections**: asteroid/debris fields stored as material percentages and spawned by RNG as the player moves through; they evolve into celestials. **Attractors/sensors**: tools that point toward rare elements.
- **FL style**: Freelancer-style fields and gas pockets.
- **Evolution Cycle**: nova → cloud → condense → pockets/clumps → chunks/regions → continents/celestials → star systems/quasars → galaxies → overload or fizzle out → repeat.
- **Black dwarf**: end-state remnant with "degenerated matter", a source of exotic rare resources.
- **SUB 3M / OVER 3M**: the two sides of the 3M system (under 3 m: pixel sprites + primitives; over 3 m: rect/circle composites). **MAIN / SUB** shapes: main body primitive vs. sub detail primitives.
- **FG / BG bleed (overflow)**: decoration may overflow its parent shape by max 4 px on top (FG) and max 16 px underneath (BG).
- **Polygon density per 4 m²**: the standard unit for tracking how much decoration detail an area has.
- **Megasheets, Starterpack, infosheets**: PSRC sprite-sheet deliverables.
- **Rayman hands/feet**: floating hands and feet with no drawn limbs, used for infant/child sprites.
- **Human stages**: baby 0–1, infant 1–5, child 5–15 (6-piece or 4-piece ALT), adult 15+ (up to 14-piece ragdoll).
- **NORM / MIN / MAX VIS LOAD / ABST**: load ranges on PS-44: normal (25 m), minimum (100 m), maximum visible load (400 m), and abstract beyond the loaded range (1 km).
- **TINY / SMALL / MEDIUM / LARGE / HUGE**: object size classes; TINY is invisible at epic-scale 50% zoom.

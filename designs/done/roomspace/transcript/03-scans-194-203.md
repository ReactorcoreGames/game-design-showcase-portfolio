## Chunk glossary

- **NVC**: most likely Nonviolent Communication; the AI's feelings/needs should cover its full feelings/needs range (scan 194).
- **Manipulator**: confirmed as the generic term for hands/grabbers; the head/mouth becomes an "emergency hand" or "unflexible grip-only manipulator" when all hands are lost.
- **Mouth hitbox collider / front collider**: a small collider at the front of the head that acts as the "hand" in emergency head mode and moves things to the mouth.
- **Packaging mediums / storage tag**: crates, boxes, barrels etc. that store items "behind" themselves the way a character's core stores items; a storage tag marks an object as (unflexible) storage.
- **Level refill equilibrium**: listed with storage, unexplained; presumably levels/rooms restocking storage toward a balance.
- **MAG/TOTAL (MAG/MAX)**: a gun's internal ammo reserve shown as magazine/total.
- **Ammobox / repairkit**: reserve items holding large amounts (8000 ammo, 2000 repair) that refill items; volatile ones gib/explode when broken.
- **HP item**: any healing item; gives a numeric HP value that the core distributes by the item's healing rule.
- **Self-damage / recoil**: items can subtract HP from their user and apply a force kick or speed burst.
- **Opinion list**: each AI NPC's personal list of opinions about every other character; "named attacks" carry an initiator identifier.
- **Regen material / core food store**: food eaten into a core reserve that heals all limbs over time; lost limbs need 10 accumulated HP to regrow and come back at 1 HP.
- **Cannibal feature**: eating decapitated limbs or dead cores with the head to heal, scaled by the remaining HP-to-gib of the eaten part.
- **Armor as sprite swap / armor as attachment**: two implementation options for armor (same collider + if statement, vs. separate collider with own HP).
- **Clothing package**: clothing comes as a package item; using it registers it with the core and applies it to the matching body sectors (4 parts: head, torso/arms/hands, legs, feet; full suits are 4-in-1).
- **Dash / tensing / retaliation**: holding Dash tenses the character, imbuing every limb and held item with CKP based on the combined velocity and angle of both parties; a victim dashing into the attacker can negate the damage ("retaliation on").
- **Naked combat**: fighting without hands/manipulators, by dashing.
- **Visitor object**: confirmed as temporary spawnables; types include dots/pixels, solids, hitscans/rays, areas/fields/zones (non-solid), lines/curves, waves, forces, emitters, projectors, spawners, nodes, trails/tracers/afterimages, all combinable into composite prefabs.
- **Node types (visitor)**: Embedded, Attached, Telepathic, Remote.
- **Blade / CKP / Hybrid modes**: CKP damage acts on contact (collision), Blade damage acts on overlap without contact, Hybrid does both (e.g. explosive particle beams).
- **Gnarliness multiplier**: a 0.0-1.0(?) multiplier in CKP damage = mass × speed × gnarliness.
- **ISD formula**: ((mass × speed) × density) × size/prominence.
- **Prominence**: a size-like property of visitor objects and gibs, affecting damage, lifetime and chaos rating.
- **Techscore**: a budget value that deployable visitors' contents must not exceed.
- **Deployable visitor**: an object that deploys either by mode swap (same object) or payload release (different object); its contents are capped by the parent's mass/fuel/techscore/density/volatility/lifetime budget.
- **Safety rating**: trading power for containment to make a deployable safer.
- **Gib/effect**: a reduced visitor object that only collides with terrain, fades with time, cannot be weaponized.
- **Chaos Intervention System / Chaos System**: performance "low effort mode": faster gib fade, template-value shortcuts, AI shock/paralysis, shader omission, delays, random erasing "anomalies", global jamming.
- **Chaos Curse**: a per-item rating (rate of fire, visitor lifetime/size/payload, projectiles per shot) that, under heavy load, makes the item jam, malfunction, vanish, explode or spawn anomalies, as an in-fiction way to cap performance cost.
- **Active Terrain objs**: terrain objects with active behaviour, which the chaos system can jam/pause.

**Conflicts and open questions**

- Scan 196 crosses out "Can grab onto ammobox, but this will not cause replenishment", and the gun note offers two refill ideas (shove/hold vs. pick up and use ammobox). Undecided; the "shove into box" version is labelled the cheap one and "not applicable if reload is in".
- Healing item distribution (scan 196): "20hp to every limb individually" vs. "20hp split across all limbs", both listed as option 1; no choice made.
- Scan 199's food regen says lost limbs need 10 HP accumulated and regrow at 1 HP; scan 196 says items can heal lost limbs directly per the item's rule. Possibly two separate systems (instant HP items vs. gradual food regen).
- Emergency head mode: scan 194 says the head "cannot throw, only drop"; scan 201 adds the head can "bite and spit out" (spit may be a form of drop/throw). Scan 194 says the head cannot store/switch items, while scan 199 says the head can eat stuff into the core, which is a kind of storing.
- Clothing parts went from 5 to 4 (scan 200: "~~5~~ 4 parts", torso upper/bottom merged), and armor also appears as both clothing package and a stat source; the sprite swap vs. attachment choice is open (sprite swap leans favoured: cheaper, armor penetration easy).
- "Because pixel art is used, only one body size is available" (scan 200) vs. scan 201's "bigger/tougher chars will have a significant advantage", which implies characters of different size/weight; perhaps non-human types or density differences.
- Dash damage examples (scan 201): 45° escape = 50% damage, 22° escape = 25% damage, which seems inverted (a smaller deflection angle giving less damage). Possibly a slip; worth flagging.
- Damage formula (scan 202): gnarliness multiplier range "0.0-1.0 ?" conflicts with the worked example (2000 → 2200 implies a 1.1 multiplier, crossed out). The multiplier range is unsettled.
- Scan 202 says "Blade acts on overlap, but does no contact", i.e. blades pass through rather than collide; check against earlier scans on blades and CKP.
- Sprite states (scan 195) say effects like muzzle flashes should be visitors, but the ammo page (scan 196) says guns emit only the projectile, no casings, for performance. Consistent with each other: minimal visitors, no cosmetic casings.
- "Pivot camera towards where you've been, not where you're going" and "Purpose/Focus: make memories, focus on past, not future" (scan 195) is a design-philosophy note squeezed onto the storage sticky; it seems to relate to the metagame/campaign generator (a trail of success/destruction documenting the journey) rather than to storage.
- "Level refill equilibrium" and "Object designated as storage — Derivates of vehicles?" are unexplained one-liners.
- The pencil settling sketch on scan 203 has no title; it sits next to Gib/Effect Types, so it may describe how gibs/debris pile up, but it could equally be about terrain material blobs (TMC) or liquids. Needs a cross-check with the terrain scans.
- Vehicles/Mounts sticky (scan 195) is a topic list only, with no detail.

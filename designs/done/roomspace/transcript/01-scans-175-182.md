## Scan 182 — Charged Kinetic Pressure

Normally, anyone and anything can slam into walls without any harm whatsoever. To deal damage, it must be active, initiated voluntarily. This applies to everything, including weapons.

① There are ~~two~~ three major values for an object's health/integrity:

1. HP – General Hit Points – (9/10 hp) → Gib if minus X hp / die if 0 hp
2. IL – Force Impulse Limit – (MAX 1000 FORCE) (Pressure) → Gib if over limit
3. JS – Joint Strength – (MAX 500 PULL FORCE) → Detach joint if over limit

① Conversely, there are three types of damage.

1. HP Damage (Charged Kinetic Pressure, Blades, Status effects (fire, poison, electricity).)
2. Impulse Stress (crushing force, focused pressure, force impacts.)
3. Pull Stress (Damaging Drag, Intentional centrifugal spin force, Ripping.)

For any of these to be damaging at all, they MUST be intentional! Same applies for Charged Kinetic Pressure.

CKP is purely for General Hit Points based damage. Blade damage is separate from CKP, but is similar in that it too deals with Hit Points only. CKP is for direct impacts mainly.

CKP's main purpose is to simulate structural damage from blunt forces/impacts. Bullets, clubs, hammers, maces, etc. Blade damage is similar, except it simulates evisceration and cutting. Swords, spikes, arrows, harpoons, saws, barbwire, sharp things.

CKP based attacks can be freely bundled with other forms of attacks, be it blade damage, impulse force or pull joint stress.

CKP is numeric, either integers or floats. Final inflicted damage may be calculated via various algorithms, taking into account parameters like: speed, charge time, mass, target density, protection, combined momentum of victim and attack.

## Chunk glossary

- **CKP — Charged Kinetic Pressure**: confirmed. Intentional, charged blunt-impact damage that deals HP damage only. Produced by charged throws (hold B), dashing (dash "weaponizes entire body with CKP"), bullets, clubs, hammers, maces. Can be bundled with blade, impulse or pull damage.
- **HP / IL / JS**: confirmed as the three integrity values on scan 182. HP = General Hit Points (gib if minus X, die at 0); IL = Force Impulse Limit (max 1000 force, gib if exceeded); JS = Joint Strength (max 500 pull force, joint detaches if exceeded).
- **Impulse Stress**: damage type 2, crushing force, focused pressure, force impacts; counts against IL. Primary damage form of crushing and of ripping at joints. Probably the same as the triage glossary's ISD.
- **Pull Stress**: damage type 3, damaging drag, intentional centrifugal spin, ripping; counts against JS.
- **Blade damage**: separate from CKP but also HP-only; evisceration and cutting.
- **Intentionality rule**: no damage unless it is active/voluntary; passive collisions (slamming into walls) are harmless.
- **Gravity drive**: the force on the torso/core that lets characters fly omnidirectionally.
- **Manipulators / manips**: hands, claws, grabbers, connectors, also mounted items/tools/guns (confirmed).
- **Core**: torso/spine; propulsion plus a back stash for items (confirmed).
- **Sensor Unit / AI Unit**: the head/brain limb type; a weak point.
- **Auxiliary Limbs**: legs, arms, tails; mass, balance, damage sponge, passive offense.
- **Mounted Item**: a limb type that is a built-in weapon/tool, a manipulator with reduced utility; cannot be dropped.
- **Vehicle Interface**: limb type (console, port, saddle, nerve bond, membrane) that lets a character transfer control to a vehicle body as pilot.
- **Switch gear**: stash current hand items into reserves and bring out others.
- **Hand Tandem / Solo (individual)**: toggle for all hands working on one item vs. each acting independently.
- **Mirror flip**: flip the whole character core to face the other way; separate from flipping items (90°/180°/270°, always clockwise).
- **MW4 groups / link-fire / group-fire / alpha strike**: MechWarrior 4–style weapon groups for multi-handed characters (future addition, not for initial release).
- **Reckless mode**: another name for Dash/Run.
- **Slipperyness / slip value**: per-hand and per-object friction value that decides grip; grip fails gradually between 500 and 800 force, is lost past 800.
- **Ripping / Crushing**: grab actions that do damage: ripping (opposite hands or pulling away) targets joints; crushing (hold LMB) is a gradual squeeze, weaker than ripping.
- **Slump / slump drag / total ragdoll mode / crouch**: grounded-ragdoll postures in gravity rooms.
- **Perf score**: performance cost per creature (examples 2 / 6 / 17 for small / medium / large animals).
- **Ghosts / Ethereals**: non-colliding sprite characters that can only spectate. **Immaterial beings**: energy/EM/exotic-matter aliens that can interact.
- **B (button)**: the let-go/throw button; tap to drop, tap + RMB to toss harmlessly, hold to charge a CKP throw.
- **BT_USER1 / BT_USER2**: placeholder bindable buttons (toggle firemode, toggle alpha strike).

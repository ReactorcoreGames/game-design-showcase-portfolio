### Left page, bottom note: TMC/Blob merging logic

- Blobs will auto-diffuse liquids/gas into cell atmos gradually (or instantly), shrinking, becoming less dense and composed only of solids.
- Blobs merge easily; if they come sufficiently close to each other, they merge both in content and momentum.
- TMCs require either welding/connecting to combine smaller TMCs together or by forcing them physically against one another to merge them into the bigger/denser TMC of the two.
- Welding any TMC into a 3x3 (max) will instead siphon the material from small to big.

### Right page, top note: TMC Critical Density Situation

As density rises past a certain threshold, it begins to gradually burn away its mass as radiation/heat via emission. Materials will transmute to heavier elements. To reach critical density, the amount of crammed material must be outpaced by the ever-increasing emission that is attempting to stabilize the TMC and its contents, hoping to reach a state of equilibrium.

Essentially, any TMC can turn into a star, black hole and a singularity.

## Chunk glossary

- **DRC (District Room Complex)**: confirmed. A wireframe/framework of subrooms immersed in a node, either embedded (stationary: cave, building, etc.) or mobile (an MDRC that entered the node). Laid out as a 16-room grid (4x4, 8x2 or 16x1). Described as the "spine / skeleton / bone structure framework" of nodes and of the universe.
- **MDRC (Mobile DRC)**: confirmed. A DRC that has been converted and launched: freighters, starships, floating/moving fortresses, mobile buildings. Moves between core nodes on the node grid.
- **Core node / core room / core DRC**: the node itself (a cell on the universe grid), its main room, and the DRC rooted at the node's center.
- **Entry room**: the DRC room that connects to the node's core room; placed with a location bias (center, corner, wall+center, etc.) plus a severity slider.
- **Spine / spineless node**: a node's rooted DRC gives it a "spine"; launching it as an MDRC leaves the node "spineless" (no DRC can be built there) until any MDRC lands/docks there.
- **Rooted / unrooted**: a subroom tied to the core node (rooted) versus converted to MDRC status (unrooted). All subrooms must be unrooted before a DRC can launch.
- **Mothball**: reverse a launch, turning a docked MDRC back into a stationary DRC at a core.
- **Docking points / dock point states**: 4 per core node room, at the corners, plus the central DRC point; each 3m (120 px). States Free (green), Reserved (yellow, "incoming"), Occupied (red).
- **Pips**: small status markers in the BG showing adjacent nodes' MDRC/DRC status.
- **Blockade / traffic jam**: 5 DRCs at a node (4 docked foreign MDRCs + 1 local) means blockade status; no more can enter.
- **Tunnel cables / anchor buoys (+ tether)**: MDRC-to-node connectors that let traffic pass constantly between an MDRC in the BG and the room; visible in the background.
- **Propulsion systems**: subroom-installed systems by medium (vacuum, air/gas, liquid) needed to move an MDRC out of a node and to launch a DRC.
- **TMC (Terrain Material Container)**: confirmed. A chunk of terrain, primarily solid (over 50%), sizes 3x3, 2x3, 2x2, 1x3, 2x1, 1x1 (in 1m units, also a 1m round one), with a composition of solids/liquids/gases and an overall density.
- **Blob**: the loose form a TMC turns into when ground/fractured; blobs merge easily and diffuse their liquids/gas into the cell atmo.
- **TMC coverings / frostings**: decorative, inert, shader-applied surface layers (snow, moss, rust, soot, etc.) applicable to TMCs, walls, objects and visitors.
- **Critical density**: the TMC density threshold past which it emits radiation/heat and transmutes; any TMC can become a star, black hole or singularity.
- **First wave materials**: the initial material set: water (snow/ice), rock, sand, mud, clay, soil (dirt), oxygen, plus dirty/contaminated/oily water.
- **Cell atmo**: the atmosphere of a single 3m room cell.
- **Wall flow / BG flow / adjacent node seep**: the three channels of atmo exchange for a room: through walls with the adjacent room, with the node's ambient background atmo, and the node's seep with neighbouring nodes.
- **Wall tech / BG panel tech**: insulation that blocks wall flow or BG flow respectively.
- **Control command systems**: installed or portable consoles in core rooms or designated (M)DRC subrooms that control adjacent nodes, room size, MDRC movement, weapons, propulsion, atmo, security, etc.
- **Nullware weapons**: a tier above antimatter, based on glitch destabilization / universal construct nullification / total reset; the only thing (besides the chaos system) that can destroy an MDRC.
- **Armageddon device**: a whole core node dedicated to one nullware device, wiping out and resetting a 10000 km³ local sector.
- **Sterilizable**: what can happen to an MDRC instead of destruction: everything inside (insulation, objects, terrain, characters, systems) can be vaporized, leaving the bare framework.
- **Core room-to-MDRC weapons/devices**: SAM-like core room systems that target MDRCs in the BG of the local and adjacent nodes (weapons, invasive alternative transit, remote modification, cargo transfer).
- **Side BG / Core's BG**: in the box-room background, the four sloping side panels show the adjacent nodes' atmo; the back wall shows the node's own (core) BG.
- **BG Type A / Type B**: two BG styles (A: repeating texture on sides; B: gradient on sides, texture on back wall).
- **Room Gradient BG System**: shader-built gradient backgrounds, dark-to-light for open areas, light-to-dark for excavated areas, value/saturation kept to the 40–60% range.
- **Beyond wall zone**: the infinite area outside the room walls visible to the camera, split into N/S/W/E segments, filled with the node's atmo/material texture, with neighbouring node atmo shown as shoreline waves at the deadzone edges.
- **Deadzone**: *(interpretation)* the camera's area beyond the room walls (the beyond wall zone), used as a clamp option for the camera.
- **Rules A–F / imprints**: universe generation levels: A universe, B galaxies, C systems, D sectors, Ea pocket planets, Eb local space, F room/subroom micro level.

## Chunk glossary

- **AI nav nodes / navnodes**: numbered waypoints forming paths/circuits, placed during room generation; AI can follow them in either direction and uses them when the direct path to a target is blocked.
- **Global lists (AI)**: shared lists the AI reads for decisions (characters, damage sources, environment awareness), each entry carrying position, velocity/direction and a power/danger rating.
- **Lifetime Points**: the "hp" of a gib object.
- **Impulse limit**: the impulse a gib can take (seems to be the IL in HP/IL/JS; confirms "IL" = Impulse Limit).
- **LMB**: left mouse button, the single use/trigger button for held items.
- **Usable item**: any held item with a function (guns, melee, tools).
- **Weaponized surface (WS)**: a surface on an item that deals damage (CKP/blade); required for an item to be a dedicated melee weapon.
- **Reckless Mode**: using an item without a WS offensively by dashing into things.
- **Emission point**: a port on an item where projectiles/attacks or exhaust come out.
- **Connector port / connector interface**: hand-grip points on an item (main and alternate).
- **Packed / Unpacked**: items fold to a compact packed form; equipping "unpacks" them onto the user, dropping repacks.
- **Volatility**: stored/compressed/trapped power in an item (ammo, capacitor, reactor); more volatility = more damage but more danger.
- **A/C/R + LM**: Ammo (reserve, dormant), Capacitor (chamber, activated ammo), Reactor (feeding, cycle intensity), Launch Mechanism (spring/magnetic); any of A/C/R can live in an external unit wired to the gun.
- **Armed matter / unarmed matter**: capacitor contents vs reserve contents.
- **Safety rating**: an item value that mitigates volatility (smart design).
- **Gnarlyness**: the damage multiplier / max potential formula (density, roughness, volatility, LMB hold charge).
- **Roughness/Hazardness**: how jagged a surface is; more ISD for blades.
- **Line visitor**: the blade of a laser sword is a visitor object of line type.
- **Pilebunker / pilebanker**: a thrusting spike weapon (both spellings used).
- **Radial context menu**: the item-switch button's four-way menu (func1/reload, prev, next, func2/purge).
- **Purge**: dropping all held items at once (func2).
- **HEV**: a piloted protective vehicle/mech (probably "Hazardous Environment" suit/vehicle, after Half-Life).
- **Head (vehicle)**: the control part a vehicle needs to work; gives limited ambient energy.
- **Linkzone / exit zone**: the area around a cockpit door used for linking the pilot in and pushing objects clear on exit.
- **Context Sensitive Operable Objects**: stationary terrain devices (elevators, doors, pumps, turrets, traps...).

- **Packing overlap.** "Packable/unpacked" items echo the character packing for doors (glossary). Here it means a held item folding into a compact form and unfolding onto the user when equipped. Worth checking whether this is meant to be one shared mechanic.
- **Fire-mode lists repeat.** Fire modes appear three times with slight differences (205 trigger sticky, 206 "Usable items: 2", 206 taxonomy flowchart).

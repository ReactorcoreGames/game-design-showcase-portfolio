## Chunk glossary

- **Room Atmo / Room Atmosphere**: the vast surrounding setting of a room node, separate from the room interior; it bleeds into adjacent atmos even when interiors are sealed.
- **Room Interior**: the small playable room, the remarkable point of interest of an area; connected to others by airlocked doors.
- **Vent cycle**: constant exchange between room interior and room atmo through every wall segment and door; can be boosted, reduced or stopped per wall segment (insulation); corners are inert.
- **Adjacent Room Atmo Effect**: periodic pass where all rooms in a local sector alter each other (minus room resistance, minus room isolation, plus greater zone influence), aiming for equilibrium.
- **Wall segment**: a standard 3 m piece of room wall; each is separate and unique.
- **Unit**: room size unit, apparently 3 m (10×10 units described as "30m²").
- **Core room / croom**: the main room of a node/district; min 3×3, max 10×10 units; must support 4 exits and one docked DRC.
- **Core room states**: Normal (untouched), Docked (district exists), Undocked (district left the area).
- **District**: each node/cell of a planet or space grid.
- **DRC (District Room Complex)**: confirmed. A grid-based (not node-based) subregion entered from a core room ("Type 2 building"), up to 16 × (10×10) rooms; an "immersible frame" that soaks and releases material/properties of the node it is docked in.
- **MDRC (Mobile DRC)**: confirmed. A DRC that can move; requires "mobility pipes/framework" in every room.
- **MicV (Micro Vehicle)**: a small single vehicle tethered like an MDRC.
- **Mobile District Tether / Tether / Anchor / Transfer wire/link**: the link between a room and a docked MDRC or MicV.
- **Entry buoy**: the in-room point where a tether to an MDRC/MicV is entered.
- **Type 1 / Type 2 buildings**: Type 1 = a room node reinforced with insulation; Type 2 = an entrance into a DRC.

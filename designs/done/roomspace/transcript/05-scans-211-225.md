## Scan 222 — Object/Item/Loose Terrain Interactions w/ Doors

- Doors have two states: (Metroid Style Doors)
  1. CLOSED - Door is essentially a solid wall segment, colliding with everything like a border would. (includes substates + locked, barred, welded or filled)
  2. OPEN - Door ONLY allow entry to PACKED characters. Everything else is treated as if the door is closed. See next page for PACKED/UNPACKED chars.
- Essentially objects cannot go in a door at all. Objects, items, terrain (loose) can only travel on a char in a chars packed state.
- Characters attempting to enter doors with held items going in at the front (unpacked state) will either be:
  - a) Behave as a wall, denying entry outright until char goes into their fully packed state.
  - b) Automatically force an unpacked char into a packed state and commit them to go through the door, overriding controls to prevent cancelling the action. In this state, the char is forcibly pulled and moved fully through the door. (And properly allighed.)
- Note! To prevent the scenario of a packed char dying mid transit into a door (and resulting in collision error of items/objects/loose terrain stuck inside or midway in a door), chars stats are suspended and collisions turned off the moment they commit to a room to room transit.
- In the event of any object managing to force itself and get stuck in a door or wall, the glitch-chaos system will try several options to resolve the issue.

## Chunk glossary

- **Hi-Dam Hit**: a high-damage hit on a border, one of the two triggers (with cumulative damage) of a Border Glitch Anomaly.
- **Border Glitch Anomaly**: when a room border takes a high-damage hit or exceeds its cumulative damage limit (e.g. 1001/1000), it breaks into a glitch outbreak (data errors, mutations, spawns, kills/heals/rez etc.), then self-seals and repairs, returning to a stable but weaker state.
- **Corner cap / Weld 1 / Weld 2 / tiling segments / stretched segment**: border construction pieces (corner piece, L joint, T joint, repeated wall segments, one stretched segment).
- **Pinned / Unpinned terrain objects**: terrain objects attached by pins to the wall, the BG, or an adjacent pinned object; they become loose once the pin breaks. States: Pinned → Ripped / Destroyed → split smaller → loose nuggets → vanish with gibs.
- **Nugget**: the smallest loose terrain piece, a ½ m sphere (loose terrain spheres range from ½ m min to 1 m max). All terrain breaks into ½ m loose spheres (equivalent exchange).
- **BG pin wall**: the close background wall that terrain can be pinned to; also acts as an atmo blocker and parallax blocker.
- **Tergen**: terrain generation.
- **Visitors / visitor types**: per-second spawned elements (dots, hitscans, solids, lines, overlap areas, rays, emitters), confirming "visitor objects" from the starting glossary; budgeted per second.
- **Perf-Budget**: 500 limbs, 100 items, 700 item-use visitors/s, 200 character-emission visitors/s, max 16 chars.
- **u (unit)**: 1u = 3 m = 120 px; 1 m = 40 px. Basic room unit/segment is 3 m, divided into a ½ m grid.
- **Segment**: a 3 m wall/room unit; room sizes are given in segments (e.g. 6×9 m = 2×3 segments).
- **Wall chunks**: count of wall pieces around a room (perimeter in metres).
- **Camera Type A / Type B**: free canvas around the room vs. view confined to room edges (black bars or themed fill).
- **Security level clearances**: content release tiers Alpha A1, Beta B2, Gamma C3, Nemesis D4, Archangel X9.
- **Border Type A / B / C**: A = 3 m wall segments with combinable Metroid doors and corner caps; B = 1×1 tile inner wall (16 tiles) inside a single solid 24-tile outer frame, with vents and dispensers; C = single collider border with exclusion zones.
- **Maxchars / Longbig wielder**: size classes of characters used for min room size data: human 1×2 m, max char 3×3 m, long big-weapon wielder 5×5.
- **Free movement space / passage space**: space level gen must keep open; characters count as passage space.
- **Point of commit / commit to transit**: the moment a packed character is committed to a door transition; after it, collisions and stats are off and the char is auto-moved, desaturated and invulnerable.
- **Metroid door / Space Chunks warp exit / Mario BG door / Touch-surface door**: the four door/exit types. Space Chunks is an outside game Reactorcore references (another standby design is also inspired by it).
- **Condition Green**: the requirement state that allows a warp exit to be used.
- **Glitch-chaos system**: confirms the starting glossary's Chaos system; it resolves objects stuck in doors or walls.

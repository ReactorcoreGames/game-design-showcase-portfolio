## Chunk glossary

- **APZ**: Auto Pack Zone, the region of a door's collider where a character is automatically packed for transit (scans 238, 239).
- **Commit line**: the line inside a door's auto pack zone; once a character's center of mass crosses it, they are committed to the transit (238, 239).
- **Zone 1 / Zone 2 (fat door)**: zone 1 is where autopacking happens and the character can still back off; zone 2 only admits packed characters (239).
- **1x / 3x doors and walls**: wall thickness in cells; a "3x fat Metroid door" needs 3-cell-thick walls but keeps momentum (239).
- **Contact surface door**: a door used by grabbing/holding E against it (239).
- **Reverse door**: a non-exit compartment that pushes content (items, liquids, critters, characters) into a room; needs reserved free space under equivalent exchange (240).
- **Glitch-Chaos system**: here explicitly the system that resolves objects that went out of bounds (237).
- **Atmo cell**: a 3 m × 3 m cell of the grid-based room atmosphere; types A corner, B wall, C open (245).
- **Single-cell room atmo / Global atmo**: alternative where the whole room is one atmo cell (244, 247).
- **Micro (local node atmo) / Macro (mega node)**: nested levels of atmo influence (247).
- **Input vent**: an emitter cell with type, rate-of-fire, pulse size and batch timing parameters (246).
- **Out vent / vent suck**: a vent that removes atmo contents (246).
- **Exit cell / entry cell**: the atmo cells on both sides of a door, which must match (248).
- **Corner caps / 3m panels**: room wall building blocks (248).
- **Area proper / Transition exits / Multi-transit / Stand alones / Transition airlock rooms**: room types (249). Area proper = 1 biome; transition exits = up to 4 biomes.
- **UG / UGWR / UW**: underground / underground water reservoir / underwater (249).
- **Safe dimensional transition shaft**: the link from orbit down into a cup world (251).
- **Remarkable location / Remarkables**: notable points of interest in a cup world (250, 251).
- **Aether**: a negative-mass, inverted (upward-opening) cup world (252).
- **Pizza world, cocoon world, pocket cup world**: world shape variants; a pocket cup world forms when a core sinks into space (253).
- **Meta distance**: abstract distance between sectors that sets how many in-between nodes connect them (253).
- **Spheres of enviro influence**: room → adjacent room → planet → universe (254).
- **BG/FG hairs**: background/foreground decoration ("hairs") as cup/pocket field modifiers *(meaning uncertain)* (251).
- **T-Object**: terrain object (257).
- **GRR**: Gory Restless Rampage Revenge, a sidetrack name (257).

## Scan Topverse Test Notes (26).jpg

Single grid-paper page: a text explanation with a worked diagram.

**Region-to-region entry determination**

- Two regions can be a different size (between ~~max~~ 25x20 vs min 10x10 tile grids) so there needs to be a way to determine where an exit tile in region A will lead to in region B. The answer is percentage based.

*Diagram:* Two rectangles. The upper-left one is region A, labelled "25 tiles" along the top ("Hori.") and "20 tiles" ("Vert.") along its right edge, which is ticked into tiles. An arrow points to a crossed tile on the right edge labelled "exit (tile #18)". A dashed line runs from that exit down to region B, a smaller rectangle labelled "22 tiles" across the top and "10 tiles" down its left edge; an arrow points to a crossed tile on B's left edge (the entry).

Calculation for A, marked "1.": "20 ÷ 100 = ~~0.2~~ = 1%" with "5" written beneath (arrows looping between 20, 100 and the 5). Then "~~[scribbled]~~ 5 × 18 = 90%", arrow down to: "Entry tile is corresponding vertical opposite side tile that is also 90% or nearest to it."

Calculation for B: "100 ÷ 10 = 10 = 1%" / "9 × 10 = 90% = Match! (or closest one)"

*(interpretation)* Reactorcore's arithmetic is written loosely: on a 20-tile edge each tile is worth 5% (100 ÷ 20), so exit tile 18 sits at 90% of the edge; on B's 10-tile edge each tile is worth 10%, so tile 9 is at 90% and becomes the entry. The rule is: express the exit's position along its edge as a percentage, then enter on the opposite edge of the neighbouring region at the tile with the same (or nearest) percentage.

## New terms

- **Lost & found list**: list of MIA characters/NPCs that get assigned to a tile/node/zone as discoverable entities for on-foot missions (Test Notes 25); the same "Lost & Found" idea as the Wilderness.
- **MIA / KIA**: Missing In Action (becomes discoverable elsewhere) / Killed In Action (scrapped into the local tile's material mass, corpse loot findable later).
- **Ejection survival kits**: parachutes, dinghies, dive coffins, stasis pods, rubber bubbles, escape pods/shuttles, airbags/balloons, tents/boxes, goo cradles.
- **Wilderness bands**: 0 entry boundary bow shock barrier, 1 light, 2 deep, 3 core, 4 extreme, X event horizon.
- **Cell tile / Area grid**: an area grid cell's contents (materials, structural element, weather, occupant) produce its biome.
- **Sub-celestial structure**: e.g. a trade lane in a star system.
- **Token Fleet Pack**: physical tokens for testing scenarios and prototyping.
- **Multi Combo Mega Game**: Reactorcore's name for the whole "30+ games all-in-one" concept.
- **MMS**: appears under "civvie/millie" in "Play random civvie/millie (MMS)"; meaning unknown.
- **Ad Circus**: main menu item; meaning unknown (perhaps an ad/promo section).
- **SoMe**: social media.
- **Evolver Devourer / EvolDevour**: the Pokemon TCG real-time monster subgame (eat, evolve, reach Eden; originally "Spore: start small, grow big", crossed out).
- **Domokun-fairy**: a flying companion acting as "trainer" / item carrier/user in Evolver Devourer.
- **Crash Derby**: modes dominate (moshpit), survive (free for all), target destroy (static/mobile), cause havok (scene).
- **spriteGenWindows.exe**: a sprite generator tool used for monster sprites.
- **AFF**: still not expanded; on Book 3 it appears as "AFF/Starsector", i.e. paired with the game Starsector as a reference point.

## New terms

- **DOC part 1–4**: Reactorcore's label for the four sticky notes on Book 10 that together document celestial layer orders.
- **Layer pictogram legend** (Book 10): orbit (specks; clear/nebula/debris, s/m/l, weather rad/wind), Hi-altitude/Void (H), Sky/Void (L), and a solid/liquid/gas grid: surface/shallows/fog, UG-soil/UW-sea/UF (under fog), bedrock/depths (UW)/deep fog, then crust/mantle/core.
- **UF**: "under fog", the gas-world counterpart of underground/underwater. **Deep fog**: the gas counterpart of bedrock/depths.
- **Area tile celestial / Region grid celestial**: the smallest celestial sizes (one 10 km² tile, or one region grid). Below 1 km² (stations etc.) an object is a "sub area tile entity instance", not a celestial. "Continent or larger have mantle."
- **CHROMO-S / PHOTO-S**: chromosphere / photosphere.
- **HAWK RAD JET**: Hawking radiation jet (black hole top layer, with the event horizon).
- **Critical effect matter**: the invented black hole core material; also neutronium, positronium/protonium "energized hyper solid", "super fluid neutrons hyper charged".
- **METAL-H**: metallic hydrogen (gas giant interior).
- **Stellar celestial grid effects**: per-star rows of zone cells in the star system grid: C (corona), H (hot), M (medium/habitable), L (low), R (radiation), G (gravity). Neutron stars emit rad-beams in |, − or + directions; black holes have "event horizon gravity overkill" G cells; anomalies affect a 1–3 range (1x1, 3x3, 5x5).
- **STAR SYS GEN VARS**: star system generator variables: star count (1–4, nebula/protostar, void), core placement (center/corner/random/side), formations (+ × − | / □ ⊏ L H), spacing (e.g. 2 spaces between), star size/type, others with params.
- **Celestial movement/combat**: moving celestials to dodge danger, ramming them into others, converting a tile/region/continent/celestial into a weapon/system/device.
- **Stars in Shadow**: a space 4X game, cited as the warpgate reference.
- **FTL cups**: unclear (Book 11, "inspired by FTL cups etc.").
- **SWs**: superweapons.
- **MV**: major vehicle (used as "MDRC/MV", "MVs/MDRCs").
- **Planetstorm vehicle movement modes**: Space/Sky/Orbit (omnidirectional thrust, free momentum), Ground/UG (no strafing, turn needs forward/back motion), Sea = Space model with high friction and drift, SUB/UW = Sea model without wind.
- **Drive Shaft node**: interior on-foot cell ("S" icon) through which the abstract drive shaft is accessed; at least one needed, more add redundancy; losing all destroys the D-shaft.
- **Connector Gantry / Special Fleet Propulsion / Travel Interdiction defensive weapons / Forward Plasma Lance/Shield**: the four drive shaft parts (A–D). **Subterrain Boring drive shaft**: drill version for UG/core/mantle travel. **Attack nullifyers**: anti-drone defences.
- **Parking space (P)**: centre of a vehicle arena ("at-location veh. spawn"); park there for on-foot missions, regional actions, FTL jumps or going "inside" a space tile.
- **Ghost mode**: exiting an arena through a door with locked path, no physics and auto acceleration (still shootable). **90° acceptable attack angle** for door entry.
- **Edge timeskip (non-FTL)**: leaving a space tile across its edge without FTL costs time. **Stasis equipment** makes long sublight trips viable.
- **"Outside" / "Inside" space tile**: outside = the tile's open-space arena; inside = celestial orbit (doors down to its area/region tiles) or, for an empty tile, a deeper spot holding star system infrastructure.
- **Cloud**: probably a star cluster (FTL is instant star-to-star in a cloud, tile by tile cloud-to-cloud).
- **Hyper (hyperspace)**: structure-based instant jump anywhere in the universe, a "one way catapult".
- **Docking trigger collider / adjustable ring spawn surface**: pack/unpack mechanics (fleet requires an active flagship, a docking gantry, max 4 ships).
- **Vehicle combat zones**: Zone 1 (orbit, upper atmo; re-entry, space exit), Zone 2 (sky, surface, soil/sea; combat area), Zone 3 (bedrock/deep sea, crust, mantle, shell, core; far depths).
- **Shell**: a layer between mantle and core (Book 15).
- **Cruise mode**: travel state in hi-atmo, space and hyperspace tunnels, subject to interdiction. **Temporary quick (interdiction) combat space**.
- **Potshot / Chaser / Anti-FTL (Direct)**: the three anti-FTL reticle types (traveller crossing, escapee pursued, invader approaching head-on). **Invader / traveller / escapee** are the target roles.
- **Chaser Interceptor Attack Reticle / Anti FTL array targetting reticle / Distance Measure / ETA / VEL / DIST**: tunnel HUD elements. **FTL medium conditions**: obstacles (gas/plasma, solids/liquids, both/all) and weather (storms, wind, bursts).
- **Size classes t, s, m, l, xl** for ships (Book 14).

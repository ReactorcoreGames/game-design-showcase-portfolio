# TopVerse notebook transcript: Book 4–9

This chunk covers six notebook pages (TopverseBook 4 to 9), each holding two to four sticky notes. Book 4 sets out the world structure: a top/side stack of vertical layers, the zoom chain from human-size room up to universe with km scales, the DRC/MDRC layers from on-foot level down to the far data node, and a building-collapse idea that Reactorcore rejects in favour of one-floor (M)DRCs. Book 5 covers on-foot room sublayers, how entities on other vertical layers are drawn ("ghosts"), vertical targeting, and alternative layer sets per celestial scale. Book 6 is a list of open topics for vehicles and regions, plus a terrain/traversal note and the chaos-system population quotas. Book 7 has a crossed-out space travel idea (replaced by a start/end tunnel system), a short topic list, max MDRC/DRC/MV size, and altitude/crash-fall rules. Book 8 covers how humans are drawn top-down (single-sprite body/head/hands plus held item), checking faces through portraits and HUD, and a "Syndemic-hijack project" note with name ideas. Book 9 (a partial spread with two notes) holds the TopVerse energy cycle diagram and user-defined "scrolls".

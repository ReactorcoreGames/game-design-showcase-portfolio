Elastomania clone
Combine: 
elastomania (bike)
human fall flat (just how the hands work in that game)
just cause 3's wingsuit/parachute/grapple mechanics
ninja warrior styled courses
soldat2d's camera view; zoomed out, small character and props
switch between free hands from human fall flat and a gun that can be used to make (cyber) ghosts go away

ideas:
spawn flat terrain, drop big obstacles, drop small props, drop start, set checkpoint lines, drop end point.
metagame is championship with a variable ghost that keeps harrasing you - allegory to doubts, haters and other mishaps, with theme of game of motivation and overcoming obstacles to fulfill your dreams despite the hardship.

V2:
Thruster Bike Transforming Robot
Bike Controls:
WS - accelerate/reverse
AD - rotate left/right
mouse 1 - boost towards this direction, mousepointer vs bike positions. Uses centered camera.

Robot Controls:
WASD fly/walk/jump/crouch
mouse 1 - kick/punch/lunge in that direction (towards mouse pointer)

Both controls:
mouse 2 - slow mo
Q - transform between bike and robot

Bike is fast and great for long distances, can smash through obstacles.
Robot can do platforming stuff better, but isn't as fast. Can also do hand-to-hand combat.

Potentially, robot has a high velocity EM accelerator pistol with autofire and autotargeting/aim-assist against distant targets, while bike is shielded during thrusts past a certain speed threshold, making it unstoppable, but also has an auto-lockon lightning gun/laser discoball that will insta-zap lesser targets around it.

Possible plot:
Vengeance/guardian rider - inspired by Biomega, play as corporate agent android Zoichi, except that he's both the bike and a robot. Take down nefarious organizations looking to take over the world by evil means. Cyberghost is a rival from an evil corp, trying to taunt you while you fight droids and turrets on your way to blow up the evil corp's mega reactor. Sort of a 90's edgy anime vibe.

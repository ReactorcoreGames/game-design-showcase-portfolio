Carrier:
Main method to warp travel to another celestial locations and to jump to another star system.
Has internal stockpiles for fuel, resources and power, but they're not disclosed to the player or metered. Player will do missions and they'll be topped up and fine automatically.
Jet hangar in middle, other functions around it. Built to be navigatable with gravity of any direction in mind but often it's just zero-g, unless carrier is grounded to planet surface for whatever reason, be it emergency flipped crash or controlled landing.
Provides healing/repairs, saves game, sanctuary, refuels/rearms, provides unlocked datalog entries archive, comms from intel officer.
Cannot be destroyed but can be temporarily rendered partially busted. Will always still self repair or have you to repair it.
Heavily armored, has proper point defenses and a few concealed heavy weapons. Tough nut to crack, somewhat like Sidonia, however it's role is to stay low profile, mask itself as an asteroid, using a gravity-well propulsion engine similar to Gargantia Chamber, being stealthy.
May be piloted and moved gradually left or right up or down roll left or right.
Design wise its shape is similar to a front view of a turtle;
+ Top half is an impenetrable shell that houses gravity well engines, warp drive, shield generator, etc. heavy machinery. This is the ships front that faces the main direction it flies in when traveling through space, deflecting, warping and withstanding most stellar hazards. Mildly inspired by Sunshine movie and the big reflective frontal solar shield of their spacecraft.
+ Lower half houses two airlocks on both sides for agent EVA exits. Usually those rarely see any use.
+ Lower half also has this big rotating turret mount (180 degree rotation range) in the center that acts both as a linear catapult launcher for the jet, the jet fabrication, repair and resupply gantry, a Gundam seed like METEOR style dock for the jet to embed itself with the turret to boost it's firepower and gain combat flight control of the carrier and as another airlock door method for agent to get back inside the safety of the carrier.
+ In theory, could allow for a reversed utopia must fall style gameplay, or something like alien invaders, or even brick breaker gameplay. Maybe even pong/destructure.
+ Besides the big turret that is the main gun of the carrier, there would also be 2-4 smaller auto laser turrets for point defence. Maybe also a gravity tractor beam that can grab things from in front of the ship and either throw them below the upper half if objects/things fly past the carrier, or to grab, drag and hold them in front of the main gun to pulverize them ruthlessly. Also maybe could work as a Moira X style gravity tractor tertiary weapon to hurl asteroids at other ships for massive damage.


Outpost:
Hangs in background, provides occasional support based on what it has available/how big the outpost is. It's worker bots usually roam the places and are bullied by local nature. No friendly fire. They smile and thumbs up you if you help them.
Against bosses, provides occasional artillery flinch sniper fire and stun missiles that keep the awakened boss busy while you make your way to attack it. Outposts will be fine. If you die outpost simply shoos away the boss when it will regenerate it's hp to almost full. The honor to kill the boss is always yours.

Wildlife:
Mostly harmless and blow up like popcorn. Usually try to get in your way. Can swarm, hurt and even kill player, but honestly cannonfodder easily dispatched by mining beam. Not an animal crime, they often mosquito/zerg tier pests that is ok to remove without worry.
Spawn from nests, but also ambiently offscreen if nests exist in local sector area. Hud shows their origin.
Neutral creatures also roam. No benefits to kill them?

Flora:
Equivalent of typical amazon forest bullshit that a machete is needed to traverse through it. Some hostile plants, traps and stationary hazard things.
Neutral flora also spawn. No benefits to erase them?

Hazards:
Gravity, sea, clouds, fog, lava, electrical clouds, gas pockets, space mines, asteroids, tornadoes, comets, singularities, radiation pulses, solar corona, etc.

Bosses (space monsters):
Compared to ones in SA, these are weakened/flawed with crappy offspring too (remnants).
That said, they're still cyberdemon tier baddies that sponge up hp and deal heavy hits that player needs to dodge and avoid. 
(Advanced) They may have an exposed weakspot(s) for optional extra +50% damage and maybe a more spectacular kill gib effect upon final hit. Kill via brute force is totally valid too, maybe a few seconds slower DPS wise, but still faster than trying to get a good angle on the weakspot.
SMs can spawn at atmo, surface, sea, under, uw.
Some can fly or swim, some can jump/dash, some can spider crawl 360 with tiles nearby, some don't move, some can only drag left or right, some can't go into space, some prefer to go to space even if they spawned underground.
SMs fire various projectile attacks and use active collision attacks too. Their projectiles and collisions may destroy blocks/tiles/props/fauna/flora.
SMs are represented by a single sprite as their main body, anything else is visual so just aim weapon at that main sprite to hit them, ignore any legs, etc.
SMs are procedurally random generated in terms of size class, material type, movement type, zone preference type (where they try to migrate from their spawn), are they an asteroids style splitter or rigid or shrinker, are they alone or a cluster of many, aggressiveness level (sml), speed, hardening, weapon power, weapon spread, weapon rapidness, weapon aoe, etc.
SMs are designed to be either fought by Agent or Jet. For SMs that remain unreachable to jet generally, they will stay and fight the agent, be considerably weaker and slower. For jet fought SMs, they will madly flee the agent, prompting agent to chase them down by boarding their jet and fighting them above surface.
SMs, remnant, fauna, flora, etc do not collide with each other at all. Each respective group also does not have friendly fire (nature which includes flora and fauna, and SMs with includes bosses and remnants, nature vs SM will hurt each other if they attack).
SMs are eerily drawn to intelligent souls much like zombies are drawn to brains. They're wild confused beasts that lack any coordination or will of their own; they merely do what they've become, a monster. For this reason they'll specifically aggro towards the player entity, be it on foot as agent or in jet as pilot or docked with the carrier, focusing on attacking the player specifically. If the player vanishes somehow from their perception, they become roaming, lost and just regenerate back to their original state they were found in, gradually. They leave everything else alone and are unable to grow into a more serious exponential threat than what they currently are. Player can vanish by dying or having their jet blown up (for bosses that focus on jet-only combat), or have the carrier become disabled and have to retreat, spend time fixing it.

Preservation goal:
The worlds that the player visits were liberated and secured by standalone units for future human expansion and colonization. Standalones worked hard to minimize the damage/corruption done to the planet as best as they could, so the inspector should continue respecting that. While regular terrain is of no consequence if it gets dug or destroyed, natural resources, ecosystems, geological support structures and ruins should be left undisturbed. Player should avoid damage to anything else than the marked objectives.

This causes the game to be more a puzzle of how to navigate a place natural chaotic with as minimal damage to the environment that you're trying to secure and protect, using your tools and weapons with utmost care.

As penalty for going against it, the player will be taxed of their supercores, revoking them one by one at thresholds or from major screwups. Incentivising good behavior to let player accumulate and stack more cores.

General gameplay:
Warp to chosen location (must complete signals/chores before can go to next one)
In carrier, get in jet, fly to surface, dig/walk to point of interest, clear it, return to jet, fly back up to carrier.
Any supercores found are automatically equipped. Visible in drop down menu. Infinitely stacking, but can only bring max 10 of them to next star system (all SC below a line will be discarded automatically, cannot reorder).b

Metagame:
Entering a star system, there are multiple planets, moons and space sectors of high interest.
Each celestial location is a room where the carrier is placed at top middle (planetary orbit) or at side middle (outskirt of space area, either side, but usually on left).
Player given a random set of signals or chores to do, with sometimes unreliable intel per location (1 common, 2 uncommon, 3 rarest)
"We need more fuel, that place seems to have some based on this signal"
It could be fuel but also could be a space monster.
Sometimes it will say that it's a space monster but it wasn't one. Sometimes it will say it's a specific type of a space monster but it will turn out to be another type entirely. Usually there will be a logical reason for the interference or intel mishap. It will say below the signal how sure the intel officer is about it. 2-100% range. Maybe with labels like the "unknown/wtf (0%), maybe(50%), highly likely(85%), absolutely sure(99.9999%).
Either way player must go there, clear each signal or chore in every location in star system before they're allowed to end the campaign to go the next star system.

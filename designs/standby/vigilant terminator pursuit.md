Racing game design idea. This is a wip doc for brainstorming a racing genre project as I figured I hadn't done one before.

The main idea is Road Rash / Road Redemption where the low metagame is about setting sights on a target as a terminator, vigilante, police, bounty hunter, angry civ, etc. to track down, pursue and intercept and a runaway bad person and either capture or kill them, wanted dead or alive.

Said bad guy usually has done something and is now on the run because they're aware they will be jailed or terminated for what they have done. Essentially the game is about bringing justice to them.

Character wise, player could be sort of like The Phantom, ghost rider, knight rider, etc., an "unkillable" badass that will pursue evildoers to the ends of the world and eventually catch up to them.

Gameplay wise I want something very similar to Road Redemption in that there's a randomized track with start and end in some biome or mix of 2-3 biomes. Player has a chosen hero vehicle and rider with certain attributes, perks, abilities and items/weapons. There are civvies/neutrals, low tier goons, heavy/elite enemies, minibosses and a big boss, as various enemy types. There's also stage events, weather, track hazards and breakable obstacles. There's also pickups and powerups. Player can kick, grab n slam, harpoon, use light/heavy melee, use one-handed guns (pistol, revolver, shotgun, SMG, grenade launcher, mines, etc.) nitro boost (quick boost, normal boost, overboost), but also use Mario Kart/Wipeout style items/weapons. Player has a health bar, nitro bar, counts for ammo/uses for weapons/items, cooldowns/conditions for abilities. Cash pickups and upgrades between stages is also good. Slomo and dodge/barrel roll ala Star Fox 64 to protect against incoming projectiles. Or a virtual cop style duck under the canopy armor cover to avoid hits to player driver, while vehicle is mostly immune to small arms fire.

Unlike many racing games, I want to have a structure/style of gameplay that isn't rooted in a traditional race type of conventions. No "get set ready go", no time limits or time extensions, no recorded time, no checkpoints, and races aren't fully contained with a single level.

Instead I want sort of a "continuous multi stage shmup" type of thing where players effort to speed up and destroy more opposition and obstacles contributes catching up to the prime target sooner. Player starts on stage 1 while boss may start on stage 2-4 and they both race in parallel, until player catches up to the boss, usually by driving faster, being more reckless, blowing shit up, launching stuff into the distances that might slow down the boss in a later stage - all accumulating to making it easier to catch up. Speed and power is rewarded. Boss may have a fear mechanic where they are more desperate if you catch up to them sooner while later they become more complacent and easier to catch.

The gauntlet of levels and catching up to the boss is designed to be feasible where player is always faster and stronger, able to shorten the distance or straight up catch up to the boss during a single low metagame campaign.

High Metagame wise, there could be a nemesis system style thing to have a single boss be a multi-step thing where each low metagame campaign gets you closer to them even if you fail to catch them during one or more campaigns. Player is relentless, but they can suffer setbacks, like fall off the track, get destroyed HP wise, go too slow, get pushed back, etc. Some let you keep going by auto transporting back to track, while others will have you reset the track or take a detour to catch up or outright fail the campaign and try another one later to attempt a new run to catch the boss you failed to get earlier. 

In some ways, there's a Time Crisis 2 style feel to the game. Player is always limited to one-handed armaments and same goes for most opponents too. Some enemies/bosses may employ slowly turning turrets. Bosses or enemies may also not be on the track, but travel parallel to it either to the side of it or flying above it or even below it during space/orbit levels.

Vehicle wise, I figure motorcycles, and smaller cars, small mecha, power armor/exosuits are what player can use/choose. Larger vehicles are reserved for NPCs, bosses, hazards/opportunities. Environments include ground, sea, air and space/orbit. Player vehicle is transformable and works in all theaters. (Like Mario Kart 8?)

Scalability wise, there's endless potential for drivers, rides, enemies, NPCs, civs/neutrals, items, weapons, stages, obstacles, props, decorations, backgrounds, roads, markings and surroundings. Bosses and the nemesis system can also have many scalable facets to it's modular content (names, mugshots, boss specific modules/enemies, maybe use player drivers/rides, but evil colors?)

High Metagame is about vengeance plots with drama and catharic justice being served. There's a trophy room of mugshots of slain/caught runners. No unlocking anything though, that's bad design. Player gets a bounty board to pick a target, a world to travel next to (player is a roaming nomad crew, similar to cowboy bebop mobile home carrier or white base from gundam, a band of unlikely characters working together to take down scum.)

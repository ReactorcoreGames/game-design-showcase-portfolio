TERRENE v3 REMIX, AAL Collector: Reach, Capture/Cute, Recover
--

Terrene Lite with heavy DKC3 inspired gamepaly and collectathon focus. Maybe with a cute monster women flavor or a regular nature wildlife version.
Mildly light hearted with stun/capture focused equipment, chill exploration vibe with reduced combat. More about grabbing, tossing and carrying stuff around like a cheeky raccoon.

Each star system creates cool gallery of recovered relics.
Relics are procgenerated amalgamations of random shapes in random configurations with random micro texture with random tints/patterns per shapes.

Bananas - trail to objective
Bear Coins - hard to reach places/miniboss defeats
Bonus Coins - small minigame challenges, extra boosters
DK Coins - major achievements, upgrades/supercores

(obvious switch them to something that makes more sense to this game)

Player Characters:
Agent
Small human operative.


Shuttle
Travel rocket from heavy cargo lander carrier to planetside and back. Can work as a submarine(?).


Carrier
Personal base of operations. (maybe) Can land on planet if need be, but slow, expensive and lumbering. Not as agile as shuttle nor fuel efficient.


BSL Megaship (maybe)
An out of reach larger colony/research/collection vessel that you are a part of. Maybe sort of like that Metroid Fusion BSL research station.
*BSL = Biological Space Labs

--

Other Characters:
Fauna/flora
Basically the same as Subnautica. Some peaceful, some territorial/evasive/retaliatory, some predatory. Different sizes. Usually you can avoid them. Can be killed, stunned or captured, dragged to carrier and delivered to BSL Megaship.
Funny idea: They're all monster women.
Capturing them needs the player to construct suitable containment vessels (slime women require glass tanks, succubi/demons need comfy beds/enchanted mirrors, vampires coffins, fish tanks, mummies/undead cloth wraps, small rodents cardboard boxes, birds wooden boxes, cats/dogs/canines tech pods, lava being stone cube, etc.)
